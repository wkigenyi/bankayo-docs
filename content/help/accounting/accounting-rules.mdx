---
title: Accounting rules
description: Save a named debit and credit account pair for posting.
---

Open **Accounting rules** from **Accounting** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"rule"). You need rule-viewing permission to see the list, create permission
to add, update permission to edit, and delete permission to remove.

A rule names one [GL account](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts) to debit
and one to credit, for an office. On [New journal](/help/accounting/journal-entries#posting-a-journal)
you can pick the rule to fill those accounts, or choose the accounts yourself.
Amounts still belong on the journal you post.

## The list

Search by name, office, or account. Export the catalog as CSV or Excel.

- **New rule** opens a sidebar: name, office, debit account, and credit
  account. Description is optional. Debit and credit must be different
  detail accounts.
- **Click a row** to edit. System rules stay visible but cannot change or
  delete.
- The row menu **Delete**s a rule that is not in use. Fineract’s reason is
  shown if it refuses.

If a rule was created with tags instead of fixed accounts, the list still
shows the tags. Saving from Bankayo replaces those tags with the debit and
credit accounts you pick.

The address bar is **Accounting › Accounting rules**.
