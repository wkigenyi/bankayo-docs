Open **Chart of accounts** from **Accounting** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"chart of accounts" or "GL"). You need GL-account viewing permission to see
the list, plus create, update, or delete permission for those actions.

A GL account is a **ledger line** — name, unique GL code, type (asset,
liability, equity, income, or expense), and usage. [Loan
products](/help/products/loans/accounting), [savings
products](/help/products/savings/accounting), and [charges](/help/products/charges)
pick **detail** accounts of the matching type. Header accounts only group
other accounts; they do not appear on those pickers.

## The list

- **Tree view** shows parent/child hierarchy. Expand a header account to see
  the detail accounts nested under it. **Expand all** and **Collapse all**
  open or close the whole chart. Search expands matching branches for you.
- **Search** by name, GL code, description, or parent.
- **Type** filters the list to one classification.
- **Export** downloads the full chart as CSV or Excel, with parent links for
  the hierarchy.

## Creating an account

**New GL account** opens a docked sidebar.

- **Name** (up to 200 characters) and **GL code** (up to 45, unique) are
  required.
- **Account type** is asset, liability, equity, income, or expense.
- **Usage** is detail or header. Detail accounts appear on product and charge
  pickers; headers group other accounts.
- **Parent** is optional. It must be a **header** of the same type. Create
  the header first if you want a hierarchy.
- **Manual entries** default to on. Turn that off when officers should not
  post journal lines here directly.
- **Description** is optional, up to 500 characters.

## Editing and deleting

- **Click a row** to edit. Disable an account so it no longer appears when
  you create a product. Fineract refuses disable if a product still maps to
  it. Disable is only on edit, not on create.
- **Delete** (row menu) asks for confirmation. Fineract refuses an account
  that has children, journal entries, or a product mapping.

The address bar is **Accounting › Chart of accounts**.
