---
title: Accruals
description: Accrue loan income through a date before today.
---

Open **Accruals** from **Accounting** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"accrual"). You need accrual-running permission to open the page and run it.

Pick a **through date** before today, then **Run accruals**. Loan income
posts through that date. The resulting lines appear under
[journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries). Fineract’s reason is
shown if it refuses.

The address bar is **Accounting › Accruals**.

This tool is for **loan** periodic accruals. Savings interest is accrued and
posted from the [savings account](/help/savings) (**Calculate interest** /
**Post interest**). Product GL accounts for that are set on
[savings product accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting).
