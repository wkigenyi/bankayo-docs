Open **Accounting** in the sidebar (a navigation group, not a page) or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "accounting". You need GL-account, journal-viewing,
journal-create, rule-viewing, financial-activity-mapping, closure-viewing,
accrual-running, or provisioning permission — if the group is missing, ask your
administrator.

Accounting lists the ledger tools you can use. Open a screen from the group
(or press **Ctrl+K** and type its name).

## Chart of accounts

[Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts) is the ledger
[loan products](/help/products/loans/accounting),
[savings products](/help/products/savings/accounting), and
[charges](/help/products/charges) pick when they post to the books. Open it
from this group (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "chart of accounts" or "GL").
Detail accounts appear on product pickers; headers group other accounts.

## Journal entries

[Journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries) is the ledger of debit
and credit lines. Search by date, office, account, or transaction. Post a
balanced manual journal — pick an [accounting rule](/help/accounting/accounting-rules)
to fill the debit and credit accounts, or choose the accounts yourself — or
reverse one. Loan movements, savings movements, and teller cash allocate or settle appear here
when they post to the books.

## Import journal entries

[Import journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-import) bulk-posts balanced
manual journals from an Excel file. Pick office, currency, and optional payment
type on the form; each row supplies date, amount, debit GL code, and credit GL
code.

## Frequent postings

[Frequent postings](/help/accounting/frequent-postings) posts a balanced
journal from a saved accounting rule when you only need office, rule, amount,
and date — the rule's debit and credit accounts are filled for you.

## Migrate opening balances

[Migrate opening balances](/help/accounting/migrate-opening-balances) posts a
balanced journal of opening GL balances for an office — one row per account with
either a debit or credit amount.

## Accounting rules

[Accounting rules](/help/accounting/accounting-rules) save a named debit and
credit account pair for an office. Pick the rule on New journal when the same
pair is posted often, or choose the accounts yourself.

## Financial activity mappings

[Financial activity mappings](/help/accounting/financial-activities) point
each system financial event — vault, teller, transfers, fund source — at a
detail GL account. Teller allocate/settle and similar postings use these
accounts.

## Closures

[Closures](/help/accounting/closures) lock an office through a date. No
journal can post or reverse on or before that date until you delete the
latest closure.

## Accruals

[Accruals](/help/accounting/accruals) accrue loan income through a date
before today. The journals appear under Journal entries.

## Provisioning entries

[Loan-loss provisioning](/help/accounting/loan-loss-provisioning) explains how
criteria, loans, GL accounts, and journal entries fit together.
[Provisioning entries](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries) calculate
loan-loss reserves from your criteria. Create an entry for a date, then post
the journals when you are ready. The journals appear under Journal entries.
