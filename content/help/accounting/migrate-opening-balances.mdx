---
title: Migrate opening balances
description: Post a balanced journal of opening GL balances for an office.
---

Open **Migrate opening balances** from **Accounting** (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "opening balance" or "migrate balances"). You need journal-create
permission to post.

Enter one row per GL account with either a **debit** or **credit** amount.
Total debits must equal total credits. The journal posts on the transaction
date you pick for the office and currency on the form.

## Before you post

- Map **Opening balances transfer contra** under
  [Financial activity mappings](/help/accounting/financial-activities) if your
  chart uses that system account for the contra side.
- GL accounts must be **detail** accounts on the
  [chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts) that allow manual
  journal entries.
- Enable a currency under [Currencies](/help/organization#currencies) if the
  currency picker is empty.

After a successful post you land on [Journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries)
with the new transaction open.

The address bar is **Accounting › Migrate opening balances**.
