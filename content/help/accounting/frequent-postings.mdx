---
title: Frequent postings
description: Post a balanced journal from a saved accounting rule.
---

Open **Frequent postings** from **Accounting** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"frequent posting"). You need journal-create permission to post.

This screen is for journals you post often with the same debit and credit pair.
Pick an office, choose an [accounting rule](/help/accounting/accounting-rules)
for that office, enter the amount and date, then post. The rule's debit and
credit GL accounts are filled automatically — you only enter the amount once,
and Fineract posts both sides at that amount.

Reference and comments are optional. Amounts use the currency **code**
(`KES`, not a symbol).

## Before you post

- The office needs at least one accounting rule with a fixed debit account and
  credit account. Rules that only use tags cannot post from here.
- Enable a currency under [Currencies](/help/organization#currencies) if the
  currency picker is empty.

After a successful post you land on [Journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries)
with the new transaction open.

The address bar is **Accounting › Frequent postings**.
