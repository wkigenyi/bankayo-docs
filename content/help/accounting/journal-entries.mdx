---
title: Journal entries
description: Search ledger postings, post a balanced journal, or reverse one.
---

Open **Journal entries** from **Accounting** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"journal" or "ledger"). You need journal-viewing permission to see the list,
create permission to post, and reverse permission to undo a posting.

A journal line is one debit or credit against a [GL
account](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts). Several lines share a
**transaction** ID. Loan disbursements and repayments, savings deposits,
withdrawals, and posted interest, and cashier allocate or settle cash show
up here when they post to the books. [Loan-loss provisioning](/help/accounting/loan-loss-provisioning)
journals appear here after you **Create journals** on a provisioning entry.
Amounts use the currency **code**
(`KES`, not a symbol).

## The list

The first view is the last month through today. Tabs switch all, manual, or
system entries. **Filters** opens a popover for dates, office, account, and
transaction ID. **Clear** restores the last month through today. **Export**
downloads every line that matches the current filters as CSV or Excel.

- **Click a row** to open every line on that transaction.
- The address bar keeps the filters so you can bookmark or share the view.

The address bar is **Accounting › Journal entries**.

## Posting a journal

**New journal** opens a wizard. You work through **Details** (office, currency,
and date; an [accounting rule](/help/accounting/accounting-rules) is optional
and fills the debit and credit accounts — Details shows those accounts as soon
as you pick the rule; reference and comments are optional), **Payment** if
payment types exist, then **Lines**. If you skipped the rule, pick the
accounts yourself. Totals must match — Fineract refuses an unbalanced journal.
**Review** shows the full entry before you post. Amounts use the currency
**code**.

If **New journal** is missing, you do not have create permission. If Post
stays off, enable a currency under [Currencies](/help/organization#currencies)
and create a detail account with **Manual entries** on.

The address bar is **Accounting › Journal entries › New journal**.

## Import from Excel

[Import journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-import) is the Bankayo
spreadsheet importer (preview, then post). Open it from Accounting next to
Journal entries, or from **Import** on this list. It is not the Fineract
workbook under Organization bulk import.

## Reversing

Open a transaction and **Reverse** when you have reverse permission. An
optional reason is stored on the reversing journal. Already reversed
transactions cannot reverse again. System postings from loans, savings, and tellers
follow the same rule — Fineract’s reason is shown if it refuses.
