---
title: Closures
description: Lock an office so journals cannot post on or before a date.
---

Open **Closures** from **Accounting** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"closure" or "closing"). You need closure-viewing permission to see the
list, create permission to add, update permission to edit the comment, and
delete permission to remove the latest closure for an office.

A closure stops [journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries) from
posting or reversing **on or before** that date for that office — manual
journals and loan or teller postings alike.

## The list

Search by office, date, comment, or who created it. Export as CSV or Excel.

- **New closure** picks an office and a date. Comments are optional.
- **Click a row** to change the comment. The office and date stay as they
  were.
- The row menu **Delete**s only the latest closure for that office. Remove
  later dates first if you need to reopen an earlier period. Fineract’s
  reason is shown if it refuses.

The address bar is **Accounting › Closures**.
