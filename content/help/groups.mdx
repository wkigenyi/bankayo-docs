---
title: Groups
description: Browse, create, and manage client groups.
---

Open **Institution → Groups** in the sidebar (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "groups").
You need group-viewing permission — if the menu item is missing, ask your administrator.

## The group list

Groups appear in a table showing name, account number, office, status, and staff.

- **Search** by name with the box above the table — results update as you type.
- **Office**, **staff**, and **external ID** narrow the list further.
- **Sort** by clicking the Name or Office column headers.
- **Pages** — use the controls under the table; you can show 10, 25, 50, or 100 rows.
- **Open a group** by clicking anywhere on its row.
- The address bar always reflects your current view — **bookmark or share the URL** and
  it reopens exactly this filtered, sorted page.

## Exporting

The **Export** button downloads the *entire* filtered list (not just the visible page)
as a CSV or Excel file. Searching or filtering first narrows what's exported. Exports
are recorded for auditing.

## Importing

**Import** downloads an Excel template you fill in, then uploads that workbook to
create groups in bulk. You need group-creation permission to upload. Download is
available to anyone who can view the list.

## Creating a group

Press **New group** at the top of the group list (you need group-creation permission
to see it). One form collects:

- **Name** and **office** — required. Members, staff, and an optional center must
  belong to this office.
- **Staff** — optional. The list is limited to employees at the selected office.
- **Center** — optional. Search for a [center](/help/centers) in the same office if
  this group sits in a village-style center. You only see this field if you can
  view centers.
- **External ID** — optional reference from another system.
- **Submitted on** — the registration date; it cannot be in the future.
- **Activate now** — active groups can be used right away. If you leave this off,
  the group stays pending until you activate it from **Actions**. Activating
  now also needs an activation date (not in the future).
- **Members** — optional. Search for clients and add them before you save.

Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). After you save, you land on the
new group's page.

## Group details

Open a group from the list. The page keeps the name, account number, status,
office, and staff at the top, then splits into sections:

- **Overview** — office, staff, external ID, and important dates (submitted,
  activated, closed) plus who recorded them when that is on file. If the group
  belongs to a [center](/help/centers), that name links to the center.
- **Members** — clients in the group. Click a name to open that client when you
  can view clients.
- **Group loans** — loans on the group itself, plus linked member loans under
  one group loan.
- **Member loans** — loans opened for members of this group.
- **Group savings** — savings on the group itself, plus linked member savings
  under one group savings account.
- **Member savings** — savings opened for members of this group.
- **Meetings** — the collection meeting used for the collection sheet.
- **Collection sheet** — attendance, repayments, and disbursements for a meeting
  date.
- **Notes** and **Documents** — staff notes and files attached to the group.
- **Extra data** — custom fields your institution attached to groups. This
  section appears only when at least one extra table is available to you.
  Sections you don't have permission to see never appear in the menu.

On a wide screen the sections sit in a left-hand list. On a tablet they become a
row of tabs.

## Members

Members must belong to the **same office** as the group. **Add members** searches
for clients and adds them in a docked sidebar. Remove a member from the row —
their own accounts are not deleted.

From a member's row menu you can:

- **Assign or change a role** — pick a group role (for example chair or secretary)
  in a docked sidebar. Removing a role does not remove the member.
- **Transfer to another group** — move the member to a group in the same office.
  You can keep the destination group's officer on their accounts and move open
  group loans with them.

You can add or remove members while the group is pending or active. Closed
groups keep their member list but you cannot change it here.

## Group loans

Loans opened on the group itself. Turn on **Show closed** to include closed
accounts. **Open a group loan** opens the same full-page application wizard as
a client loan (product, optional external ID, terms, charges, schedule, review), without collateral
or guarantors — those need a client. The header shows this group's name on every
step. The first step defaults the officer to the group's staff when they are in
the list, and can attach the collection meeting so repayments follow meeting
dates. You can also link group savings when a group savings account exists. The
group must be active. Close (X) in the top bar returns you to this list;
**Back** and **Continue** sit at the bottom. After you submit, you land on that
loan. The loan header names this group and links back here.

## Linked member loans

**Open linked member loans** opens a full-page wizard: choose a product and
terms, then tick members and enter each principal. The header shows this group's
name. If the product sets a minimum or maximum principal or interest rate, that
range appears under those fields. That creates one loan per selected member, tied
together as a group loan. The group must be active and have members. After you
submit, you return to Group loans. Linked child loans open on the loan workspace.

## Member loans

Loans opened for members of this group. Turn on **Show closed** to include
closed accounts.

To open a **joint-liability loan** for a member, pick that client at the bottom
of the page. You land on that client's open-account wizard with the group
already linked. The header shows that client's name — the loan is on the member,
not the group. The group must be active.

## Group savings

Savings opened on the group itself. **Open group savings** opens a docked
sidebar (required product and date, optional product overrides) — the group
must be active. Cancel closes the sidebar. After you submit, you open that
account’s [savings workspace](/help/savings). Click a row to open the same
workspace.

## Linked member savings

**Open linked member savings** opens a full-page wizard: choose a product and
date, then tick members. The header shows this group's name. That creates linked
savings for those members under one group savings account. After you submit, you
return to Group savings. Linked child accounts open the
[savings workspace](/help/savings).

## Member savings

Savings opened for members of this group. Open savings for a member the same
way as a joint-liability loan: pick the client at the bottom of the page. That
opens the savings sidebar on that client. Existing member savings open the
[savings workspace](/help/savings).

## Meetings

A collection meeting is the repeating calendar this group uses for collection
sheets. **Add meeting** collects a short title, start date, and optional
repeat rule (weekly needs a weekday; monthly needs which week of the month).
The meeting type is always a collection meeting.

If this group belongs to a center that already has a collection meeting, that
calendar is **inherited** and read-only here. Change it on the center instead.

Delete a meeting you created on the group when you no longer need it. You
cannot generate a collection sheet until another collection meeting exists.

## Collection sheet

The sheet table is empty until you generate. **Generate** opens a sidebar
for the meeting date (usually the next eligible date). Submit to load
clients, attendance, and amounts. Mark attendance, enter repayment amounts,
and enter disbursements when a loan is ready to disburse. **Save collection
sheet** on the results toolbar posts those amounts. You need permission to
save the sheet.

If there is no collection meeting yet, add one from **Meetings** first.
The institution-wide [collection sheet](/help/collection-sheet) finds every
center meeting that day. Individual (non-JLG) loans use the
[individual collection sheet](/help/individual-collection-sheet).

## Notes

Staff notes on this group. Add, edit, or delete a note from this section.
Notes are visible to staff who can view group notes.

## Documents

Files attached to this group. Upload a file with a name (and optional
description), download it from the row, or delete it. A new upload needs a
file; editing can keep the existing one. Files can be at most 10 MB.

## Extra data

Custom fields your institution attached to groups. Each table is listed
separately. Add or edit in a docked sidebar; delete asks you to confirm. A
table that allows more than one row lets you add another. Required fields are
marked with an asterisk (*). This section is hidden when no extra tables are
available to you.

## Editing and status

**Edit** changes the name or external ID in a docked sidebar. The office cannot
be changed after create. Assign or unassign staff from **Actions**, not Edit.

**Actions** depend on status and your permissions. Commands that need a date or
other details open a docked sidebar; delete and unassign staff ask you to confirm.

- **Pending** — edit, activate, assign or unassign staff, or delete when the
  group has no members. Activation can be refused if there are too few or too
  many members — the message explains why.
- **Active** — edit, close (date and reason), assign or unassign staff.
- **Closed** — the group is read-only here.

If a command is not allowed, the reason from the core banking system is shown.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No groups yet" or "No matching groups" | Create a group, or clear the search and filters. |
| "Couldn't load groups" | Press Retry; if it persists, the core banking system may be unreachable — tell your administrator. |
| Groups menu missing | Your role lacks group-viewing permission. |
| Activation refused | Check the member count and the message; then try again. |
| Member add refused | The client must belong to the same office as the group. |
| Center search empty | The center must belong to the same office, and you need center-viewing permission. |
| Collection sheet empty | Add a collection meeting first, then generate for a meeting date. |
| Inherited meeting | The calendar comes from the group's center — change it there. |
| Extra data section missing | No extra tables are registered for groups, or your role cannot view them. |
