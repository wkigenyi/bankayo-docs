Open **Shares** to see your ownership in the SACCO.

## Holdings summary

The shares card shows:

- **Approved shares** — units staff have already confirmed
- **Pending shares** — purchase requests waiting for approval
- **Price per share**
- **Total value** of approved holdings

**Share Transactions** lists purchases and redemptions with status and dates.

## Buy shares

1. Tap **Buy Shares**.
2. Review **unit price**, **current shares**, **savings available balance**, and
   any **portfolio limit**.
3. Self Help shows how many **purchasable units** you can buy (the lower of
   balance ÷ price and remaining room under the product maximum).
4. Enter the number of shares and check the **investment amount**.
5. Tap **Confirm Purchase** when ready.

Purchases use your **savings** balance. New requests stay **pending** until
SACCO officers approve them. You are notified when they are approved, and the
units move into your approved total.

If you already have pending shares, Self Help **blocks another purchase** until
those are approved. If you have reached the product **portfolio maximum**, the
form explains that you cannot buy more until the limit allows.

On the purchase screen, **Confirm Purchase** is the primary action and **Back**
is secondary.

Walkthrough recordings stop before **Confirm Purchase** so live accounts are
not changed.
