**Self Help** is the Bankayo member app — the phone-first experience for SACCO
members to check balances, apply for loans, buy shares, and manage their
profile. Staff tools stay in the operator workspace; this section is for members.

## Topics

- [Signing in](/help/self-help/signing-in) — pick your SACCO and sign in
- [Home](/help/self-help/home) — balances, quick actions, and recent activity
- [Savings](/help/self-help/savings) — deposits, withdrawals, and statements
- [Loans](/help/self-help/loans) — apply for a loan and track applications
- [Shares](/help/self-help/shares) — buy shares and review pending purchases
- [Profile](/help/self-help/profile) — appearance, password, and sign out

Every screen in the app has a **Help** entry that opens the matching page here.
