**Home** is the first screen after you sign in. It summarizes your savings,
loans, and shares, and offers shortcuts for common tasks.

## What you see

- **Savings balance** — available funds in your primary savings account
- **Loans** — active and pending loan counts and outstanding balance
- **Shares** — approved and pending share holdings
- **Quick actions** — shortcuts such as deposit, withdraw, apply for a loan, or
  buy shares
- **Recent transactions** — latest savings activity

Use the bottom navigation (Home, Savings, Loans, Shares, Profile) to move
around the app.
