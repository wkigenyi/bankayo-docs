Self Help starts with your **organization**, then the **username** and
**password** from your SACCO administrator.

## Pick your SACCO

1. Open the Self Help app (or the web URL your SACCO shared).
2. Search for your SACCO by name (type at least two characters).
3. Select it from the results.

Only SACCOs **registered with Bankayo** appear in search. If you cannot find
yours (and the spelling is correct), the SACCO is **not onboarded** yet. Ask
them to reach out to Bankayo — we welcome organizations that want Self Help for
their members.

## Sign in

1. Enter the **username** from your SACCO administrator (not necessarily your phone number).
2. Enter your **password** (also from your SACCO administrator).
3. Optionally turn on **Remember me** on a personal device.
4. Tap **Sign in**.

After a successful sign-in you land on **Home**.

## Need help signing in?

- No credentials yet — ask your SACCO administrator for Self Help username and password.
- Forgot password — ask SACCO staff to reset it in the operator workspace.
- Wrong SACCO — go back and pick the correct organization before signing in.
- Locked out — staff can unlock or reset your Self Help user.
