Open **Loans** to see your borrowing with the SACCO and — when enabled — to
start a new application.

## Your loan categories

Loans are grouped into three tabs:

1. **Pending** — applications you submitted that staff have not finished reviewing.
2. **Active** — approved and disbursed loans; balances and next installments appear here.
3. **Closed** — fully repaid or otherwise closed by your SACCO.

The summary at the top shows active loan count and outstanding balance.

## Apply for a loan

Available only when your SACCO allows Self Help loan applications.

1. Tap **Apply for Loan**.
2. **Select a product** — only products your SACCO enables for members appear.
   Each card shows interest, **amount range**, **period range**, and whether
   **guarantors** are required.
3. Enter **amount** and **period (months)** within those limits.
4. Optionally add up to **two guarantors** (full name and 14-character NIN).
   Some products require guarantors; otherwise you may leave them blank.
5. Tap **Review Schedule** to preview installments.
6. Tap **Submit Application** when you are ready (you can go back to edit first).

## After you submit

1. Your **SACCO administrator** is notified of the new application.
2. **Loan officers** review it (including guarantor details when provided).
3. If they **approve** and **disburse**, you receive a **notification**.
4. The loan moves from **Pending** to **Active** on your Loans screen.

Walkthrough recordings stop on the schedule preview and **do not submit** live
applications.
