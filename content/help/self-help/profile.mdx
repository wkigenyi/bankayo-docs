Open **Profile** from the bottom navigation. You see your name, office, and
settings for this device.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**. System follows your phone or computer.
Light or Dark overrides the device theme until you change it again.

## Help

**Help** opens Bankayo’s Self Help documentation in your browser — written steps
and any published video walkthroughs for the screen you are on.

## Change password

1. Tap **Change Password**.
2. Enter your **current password**.
3. Enter a **new password** (at least eight characters) and confirm it.
4. Submit.

After a successful change you may need to sign in again, depending on your
SACCO’s identity provider.

## Sign out

Tap **Logout**, then confirm. That clears your session on this device. Use
**Cancel** if you opened the dialog by mistake.

To use a different organization, sign in again and choose another SACCO.
