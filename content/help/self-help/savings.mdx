Open **Savings** from the bottom navigation to see your account with the SACCO.

## Balances

The savings card shows:

- **Running balance** — overall account balance
- **Available balance** — what you can use now
- **Last transaction date**
- **Interest earned** — when your SACCO pays interest
- **Held as collateral** — amounts locked as loan security (when applicable)

**Recent Transactions** lists deposits, withdrawals, and other movements with
running balances.

## Statement

Tap the statement control, choose a **from** / **to** date range, and download a
**PDF**. Statements are for your records; they are only fully valid with your
SACCO’s seal or authorized signature.

## Deposit (mobile money)

1. Tap **Deposit** (floating action on mobile).
2. Enter an **MTN** or **Airtel** Uganda phone number (the network is detected).
3. Enter an **amount** within the shown minimum and maximum.
4. Tap **Continue**.

Mobile money deposits require your SACCO to be registered with **MTN MoMo**,
**Airtel Money**, or **Safaricom**. Ask your administrator — once registration
is in place, Bankayo can activate deposits for members. Until then, Self Help
explains what to ask staff rather than charging a live wallet.

## Withdrawal

1. From **Home**, tap **Withdraw**.
2. Select the savings account.
3. Enter an amount up to your **available balance**.
4. Submit the request.

Withdrawals are sent for **SACCO staff approval** before funds are released. You
are notified when staff act.

Walkthrough recordings stop before a live withdrawal submit. Deposit continue
only shows the provider-registration guidance screen.
