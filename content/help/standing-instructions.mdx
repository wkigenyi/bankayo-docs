---
title: Standing instructions
description: Schedule recurring transfers between accounts, or automatic loan repayments from savings.
---

Open **Standing instructions** in Institution (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"standing instruction"). You need **READ_STANDINGINSTRUCTION** to see the
list, **CREATE_STANDINGINSTRUCTION** to add one, and update or delete
permission to change or remove it.

A standing instruction is a schedule. Each run posts an
[account transfer](/help/account-transfers). This is not the
standing-instruction-at-disbursement flag on a new loan, and not repay from
savings on a loan workspace.

## What you can do

**Filters** narrow by client name or transfer type. Types come from the
Fineract standing-instruction template (account transfer and loan repayment
today; charge payment only if the template includes it). **Export**
downloads the filtered list. **Run history** opens
[Organization → Standing instruction history](/help/organization/standing-instructions-history).

**New instruction** is a five-step wizard: from, to, instruction,
recurrence, review.

- **Account transfer** cannot involve a loan — both sides must be savings.
- **Loan repayment** must be savings to a loan.
- A savings destination must use a **fixed** amount and **periodic**
  recurrence.
- Periodic recurrence needs an interval and frequency. Monthly or yearly
  also needs the day and month (`dd MM`).
- **Dues** amount is only for loan repayment, not savings-to-savings.

Open a row to see associated transfers. **Edit** can change amount, dates,
status, priority, instruction type, and recurrence — not the from/to
accounts or the name. **Delete** stops future runs; posted transfers stay
on the accounts.
