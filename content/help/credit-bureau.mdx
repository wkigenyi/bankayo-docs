title: Credit bureau

Open **Credit bureau** in Institution (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"credit bureau"). You need **READ_CREDITBUREAU** or one of the credit-bureau
create/update permissions. Superuser roles with **ALL_FUNCTIONS** can always
open it.

This is Fineract's **configuration** for a credit bureau. It is not a client
credit profile and not the Mifos **CB-ILD** plugin (that talks to a separate
server). Fetching or storing a report uses `/creditBureauIntegration` and a
live bureau — Bankayo does not fake that product here.

## Organisation

The first tab lists organisation mappings. Fineract ships a small catalog
(today **ThitsaWorks** for Myanmar). **Add bureau** picks one of those, sets
an alias (at most 100 characters), and whether it is active. After create,
Fineract can only turn the mapping on or off — click a row to change
**Active**.

## Loan products

Map a loan product to an organisation bureau. Products come from
`GET /CreditBureauConfiguration/loanProduct`. Create collects whether a
credit check is mandatory, whether to skip the check if the bureau is
unreachable, and a **stale period** in days (greater than zero). Edit can
only change **Active**.

## Configuration

Keys the integration reads (Username, Password, token URL, and similar).
Key, value, and description are each required and at most 100 characters.
Edit cannot change the key.

**Export** on each tab downloads CSV or Excel.

The address bar is **Credit bureau**.
