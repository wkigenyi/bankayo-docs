title: Recurring deposits
description: Open a recurring deposit in its own workspace.
---


A recurring deposit has its own workspace — not the client header or section list.
Open one from a [client’s Recurring deposits section](/help/clients#client-details).
The product that account was opened on is defined under
[Recurring deposit products](/help/products/recurring).

The top of the page keeps the account number, status, account type (with an
icon), product, a link to the client or group this account belongs to, and the
balance, then splits into sections:

- **Overview** — performance (balance, available, deposits, withdrawals, posted
  interest, recommended installment, and maturity amount) next to product, deposit
  period, lock-in, compounding and posting, collection frequency, whether
  withdrawals are allowed, and whether the installment is mandatory. Overdue
  amounts appear when installments are behind. Amounts use the currency **code**.
- **Transactions** — postings on the account, 25 to a page (you can choose 10,
  50, or 100). Filter by transaction date and type; the address bar keeps those
  filters. Reversed postings are hidden until you turn on **Show reversed**.
  Cash deposit and withdrawal rows can be undone or modified when the account
  is active. Account-transfer rows cannot be undone here. Cash deposit and
  withdrawal rows that are not reversed can download a receipt PDF.
- **Statement** — preview a date range of postings and download a PDF. See
  [Recurring deposit statement](/help/recurring/statement).
- **Charges** — add, pay, waive, or stop fees and penalties. See
  [Recurring deposit charges](/help/recurring/charges).

**Actions** depend on status and your permissions. Commands that need a date,
amount, or payout option open a docked sidebar over the account — the header
stays visible. Confirm-only commands (calculate or post interest, undo) ask you
to confirm. Cash deposits and withdrawals list only institution payment types —
not system-generated ones.

Typical work from the recurring deposit header:

- **Pending** — approve, reject, or withdraw the application.
- **Approved** — activate, or undo approval.
- **Active** — record a **deposit** or **withdrawal** (withdrawal only when the
  product allows it), calculate or post interest, **change the recommended
  deposit** amount from a date, and **close early** (premature close). Early
  close asks Fineract for the amount due on the chosen date, then lets you
  **withdraw** it or **transfer** it to a savings account (the options Fineract
  returns).
- **Matured** — **close** with the same payout options, plus reinvest principal
  and interest or principal only when Fineract offers them.

From a client list, clicking a recurring deposit row opens this same workspace.
Fixed deposits use the [fixed deposit workspace](/help/fixed).

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Open a recurring deposit from a client" | Click a recurring deposit on a client. |
| Account missing or not loading | It may have been closed, or your role may not include recurring deposits — tell your administrator. |
| No withdrawal action | The product must allow withdrawals on this account. |
