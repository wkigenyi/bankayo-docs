---
title: Recurring deposit charges
description: Add, pay, waive, or stop fees and penalties on a recurring deposit.
---

The recurring deposit workspace has a **Charges** section. Open it from an
account — there is no institution-wide deposit charge list. The **?** button on
that section opens this page.

A charge on an account is money: an amount due, paid, or waived. The template it
comes from lives under [Products → Charges](/help/products/charges). Collection
time and calculation are on that template, not here. A
[recurring deposit product](/help/products/recurring) can attach templates so they
start on every new account; you can still add more from this section.

Charges use the same Fineract savings-charge resource as
[savings charges](/help/savings/charges)
(`/recurringdepositaccounts/{id}/charges`). You need the savings-account-charge
permissions to add, pay, waive, stop, or remove them.

## The table

Each row is a charge on this account: name, fee or penalty, when it is
collected, due date, amount, paid, outstanding, and status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Outstanding** | An amount is still due. |
| **Overdue** | The due date has passed and an amount is still due. |
| **Paid** | Nothing is left to collect. |
| **Waived** | The outstanding amount was written off. |
| **Stopped** | A recurring annual, monthly, or weekly fee is no longer collected on this account. |

## Add a charge

**Add charge** opens a docked sidebar. You need permission to create a savings
account charge, and the account must not be closed or rejected.

Pick a savings charge in this account’s currency. Amount defaults from the
template and can be changed. Extra fields depend on collection time:

- **Specified due date** — a **due date**.
- **Annual fee** and **monthly fee** — a **due day** (day and month).
- **Monthly fee** and **weekly fee** — how often the fee repeats (months or
  weeks).

If the picker is empty, create an active savings charge first under
[Charges](/help/products/charges), in the same currency as this account.

## Pay

On an **active** account, **Pay** collects the outstanding amount from this
deposit account. The sidebar asks for a **payment date**, the **amount**, and
an optional **payment type** if your institution uses them. Cash types are
institution payment types only — not system-generated ones. Add those types
under [Organization → Payment types](/help/organization#payment-types).

You cannot pay a charge that is already paid, waived, or stopped.

## Waive

**Waive** writes off the outstanding amount so it is no longer due. The account
must be active and the charge still outstanding — the same rule as pay.

## Stop recurring

**Stop recurring** is only for an **annual**, **monthly**, or **weekly** fee
that has **no** amount still due. It stops collecting that fee on this account.
It does not appear on a specified due-date charge, or while an amount is still
outstanding — pay or waive first.

## Remove

**Remove** is only for a charge on a **pending** application that has not been
paid or waived. Confirm in the dialog. After the account is approved or
activated, you pay, waive, or stop instead of deleting.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Empty charge picker | Create an active savings charge in this currency under [Charges](/help/products/charges). |
| No **Add charge** | The account is closed or rejected, or your role cannot create savings account charges. |
| No pay / waive | The account must be active and the charge still outstanding. |
| No **Stop recurring** | Only annual, monthly, or weekly fees with nothing outstanding can be stopped. |

See [Recurring deposits](/help/recurring) for the rest of the workspace. Fixed
deposits use the same charge actions on
[Fixed deposit charges](/help/fixed/charges).
