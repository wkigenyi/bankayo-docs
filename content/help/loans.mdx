Open **Institution → Loans** in the sidebar (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "loans").
You need loan-viewing permission — if the menu item is missing, ask your administrator.

## The loan list

Loans from across the institution appear in a table showing account number, client
or group, product, and status. A group-only loan names the group in the Client
column.

- **Search** by account number or external ID with the box above the table.
- **Sort** by account number when you are not searching.
- **Pages** — use the controls under the table; you can show 10, 25, 50, or 100 rows.
- **Open a loan** by clicking anywhere on its row.
- The address bar always reflects your current view — **bookmark or share the URL**.

Search uses Fineract’s loan search (account number or external ID). Opening a *new*
loan still starts from an active [client](/help/clients#opening-an-account).

## Exporting

The **Export** button downloads the *entire* filtered list (not just the visible
page) as a CSV or Excel file. Searching first narrows what's exported. Exports
are recorded for auditing.

## Loan details

A loan has its own workspace — not the client header or section list. The top of
the page keeps the account number, status, account type (with an icon), product,
a link to the client or group this loan belongs to, and outstanding balance, then
splits into sections:

- **Overview** — product, balances, interest, and important dates. Amounts use
  the currency **code**. A group loan also names the group and links to it.
  When the loan uses a down payment, overview shows the percent of the
  disbursed amount and whether Fineract auto-repays it on disbursement.
- **Transactions** — postings on the loan, 25 to a page (you can choose 10, 50,
  or 100). Filter by transaction date and type; the address bar keeps those
  filters. Reversed postings are hidden until you turn on **Show reversed**.
- **Schedule** — the disbursement (amount paid out, tagged **Disbursement**)
  and repayment installments. Each repayment is **Paid**, **Due** (due today),
  **Overdue**, or **Upcoming**. A period with nothing to collect is **Nothing
  due**, not Paid. A down-payment installment is tagged **Down payment**.
- **Charges** — fees and penalties on this loan. Add a charge from templates
  defined under [Charges](/help/products/charges). Specified due-date charges
  need a due date. You cannot add charges on a closed, overpaid, or written-off
  loan.

**Actions** depend on status and your permissions. Commands that need a date,
amount, or other details open a docked sidebar over the loan — the header stays
visible. Undo and delete ask you to confirm. Fineract checks dates and amounts;
if a command is not allowed, the reason from the core banking system is shown.

Typical work from the loan header:

- **Pending** — approve, reject, withdraw, or delete the application; assign
  a loan officer; [edit the repayment schedule](#editing-the-repayment-schedule)
  when the product allows variable installments.
- **Approved** — disburse, or undo approval. If the loan has a linked savings
  account and your role allows it, the disbursement form lets you send the
  money there instead of cash.
- **Active** — record a repayment (or a down payment if the product uses
  one and it is not auto-repaid on disbursement), waive interest, close,
  write off, charge off, or foreclose; [reschedule remaining
  installments](#rescheduling-remaining-installments); undo
  disbursal (or the last tranche on a multi-disburse loan). Repayment can
  transfer from the customer’s active savings (the default when your role
  allows it) or be recorded as cash. A transfer cannot exceed the savings
  available balance or the loan outstanding. Cash repayments and other cash
  loan payments list only institution payment types — not system-generated
  ones. Add those types under
  [Organization → Payment types](/help/organization#payment-types).
- **Written off** — undo the write-off or post a recovery payment.
- **Overpaid** — refund the credit balance.

From a [client’s Loans section](/help/clients#client-details), clicking a row
opens this same loan workspace.

## Editing the repayment schedule

**Edit repayment schedule** is for a loan that is still pending approval, on a
product that allows variable installments. It is not the same as
[rescheduling remaining installments](#rescheduling-remaining-installments) on
an active loan.

You need **CREATESCHEDULEEXCEPTIONS_LOAN** to save, **READ_LOAN** to validate,
and **DELETESCHEDULEEXCEPTIONS_LOAN** to recover the original schedule.

From the loan header:

1. Change an installment amount or due date, or delete an installment you do
   not want. Deleted rows can be restored before you save.
2. Optionally apply the same amount to a range of remaining installments.
3. **Validate** so Fineract can rebuild interest and outstanding principal.
4. **Save schedule** to keep the variations on this application.

Due dates stay between neighbouring remaining installments (deleted neighbours
are skipped) and after the expected disbursement date. If remaining principal
no longer covers the loan amount, validate or restore a deleted installment.

**Recover original** removes previously saved variations and restores the
schedule Fineract built when the loan was submitted.

## Rescheduling remaining installments

**Reschedule** rebuilds unpaid installments on an active loan. It is not the
same as **Close as rescheduled**, which closes the account.

You need **CREATE_RESCHEDULELOAN** to submit a request, **READ_RESCHEDULELOAN** to
preview it, and **APPROVE_RESCHEDULELOAN** / **REJECT_RESCHEDULELOAN** to decide.
The reason list comes from Fineract’s `LoanRescheduleReason` code (the
reschedule template).

From the loan header:

1. Choose the unpaid installment to start from (its due date).
2. Pick a reason and the submitted-on date.
3. Turn on at least one change: a new due date, mid-term grace, extra
   repayments, or a new interest rate. You can also ask Fineract to
   recalculate interest.
4. Submit. The loan schedule does not change until the request is approved.

A pending request shows **Review reschedule**. That sidebar previews the
rebuilt schedule. Approve or reject with a date; Fineract will not apply a
decision dated before the request was submitted.

Fineract rejects a start date that is not an unpaid installment, charged-off
loans, and some multi-disburse tranche products. Progressive-schedule loans
may allow only one kind of change at a time.

## Opening a loan

Open a loan from an [active client](/help/clients#opening-an-account) (or a
group / JLG member). The wizard collects what Fineract accepts on
`POST /loans` for that product — fields the product cannot use stay hidden.

- **Linked savings** (client loans only) — optional active savings of this
  client. Group-only loans do not link a client savings account.
- **Standing instruction at disbursement** — each installment is then
  collected from the linked savings when it falls due, so repayments stay
  on schedule without a cash visit.
- **Account-transfer charges** also need that linked savings account.
- **Product-gated terms** appear when the product allows them: a fixed
  installment amount (EMI), equal amortization, monthly nth-day repayment,
  partial-period interest, multi-disburse tranches, down payment, top-up,
  progressive-schedule extras, and installment-level delinquency when the
  product has a bucket.
- **Schedule preview** does not persist. Recalculate after you change terms
  or charges if you want updated figures. Fineract still calculates the
  repayment schedule when you submit. Preview does not send linked-account,
  standing-instruction, account number, or note fields.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No loans yet" or "No matching loans" | Open a loan from a client, or clear the search box. |
| "Couldn't load loans" | Press Retry; if it persists, the core banking system may be unreachable — tell your administrator. |
| Loans menu missing | Your role lacks loan-viewing permission. |
| Edit repayment schedule missing on a pending loan | The product must allow variable installments, and your role needs **CREATESCHEDULEEXCEPTIONS_LOAN**. |
| Reschedule missing on an active loan | Your role lacks **CREATE_RESCHEDULELOAN**, or a request is already pending. |
| "Repayment schedule installment does not exist" | The start date must be an unpaid installment due date. |
