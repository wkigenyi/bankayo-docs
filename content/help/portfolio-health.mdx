**Portfolio health** is the analytics companion to the home
[Dashboard](/help/navigation). Pick an office (the same control as on the
Dashboard) to see portfolio-at-risk, outstanding by loan product, and how much
outstanding sits in each days-past-due band.

## What the figures mean

- **PAR 30 / 60 / 90** — outstanding of **active** loans that are at least 30,
  60, or 90 days past due, as a share of total outstanding. A loan that is one
  day late is not PAR 30.
- **Days past due** — non-overlapping bands (1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90) using
  the same overdue-since date against the business date when that feature is on.
- **Product mix** — outstanding on active loans, grouped by product.

Amounts use the organisation currency code. The page is hidden if your role
cannot read loans.
