---
title: Recurring deposit products
description: Term, amount, interest chart, mandatory deposits, and withdrawals.
---

Open **Products → Recurring deposits**. **New** and **Edit** use the
same wizard. Open a product for a sectioned workspace (general, terms,
settings, interest chart, charges, and accounting when the rule is not
None). Terms show the length and unit together — for example **3 months**.

Like [fixed deposits](/help/products/fixed): term, deposit amount range,
interest settings, lock-in, premature-closure penalty, tax, optional
charges, and one interest chart with consecutive rate tiers. Recurring
products can also require a mandatory deposit, allow withdrawals, apply
advances to future installments, and set a minimum balance for interest
(that last field is accepted only on recurring products).

Chart dates belong to the rate schedule. Recurring products have no product
start or close date.

Amounts show the currency **code**. Required fields are marked with an
asterisk (*).

When an officer [opens a recurring deposit](/help/clients#opening-an-account),
the docked sidebar starts from this product. **Required** is the product,
dates, recommended installment, term, and collection frequency; **Optional**
can still change interest, lock-in, mandatory/withdrawal flags, tax, and
charges for that one account. Maturity instruction is stored on the account
— if it is left unset, the officer chooses it when closing.

## Wizard steps

1. **Details** — name, short name, description.
2. **Currency** — code, decimal places, multiples.
3. **Terms** — default deposit amount, optional min/max amount, minimum
   and maximum term, optional term multiples, compounding, posting, and
   calculation. Compounding cannot be longer than posting.
4. **Settings** — lock-in, premature-closure penalty, tax, whether deposits
   are mandatory, whether withdrawals are allowed, and whether advances
   apply to future installments.
5. **Interest chart** — same consecutive-tier rules as fixed deposits,
   including optional amount grouping.
6. **Charges** — optional savings charges in this currency.
7. **Accounting** — None, cash, or accrual periodic, and the GL accounts
   that rule needs. See [Fixed deposit products](/help/products/fixed) for
   the exact account list.
8. **Mappings** — optional payment-channel and charge-to-income mappings
   when accounting is not None.
9. **Review** — confirm every section, then create or save.
