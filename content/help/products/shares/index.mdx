title: Share products

Open **Products → Shares** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "share
products"). You need permission to view share products — if the menu item is
missing, ask your administrator.

A share product is the template for every share account opened on it. It sets
the currency, how many shares exist, the unit price, how many shares a client
can hold, optional lock-in and dividend rules, market-price history, attached
charges, and — when books are kept in Bankayo — the general-ledger accounts
those movements post to.

## The list

Each row is a product: name, short name, currency, and total shares.

- **Search** with the box above the table.
- **New** starts the [create wizard](/help/products/shares/create) when your
  role allows it.
- **Open** a product by clicking its row.

## Opening a product

The product opens as a workspace with a section rail — the same pattern as a
loan or savings product.

- **General**, **Terms**, and **Settings** are always there.
- **Market price**, **Fees**, and **Accounting** appear only when the product
  has them. Accounting is there when the rule is Cash.
- Charge names link to the [charge](/help/products/charges).
- Accounting lists GL accounts by type (assets, liabilities, equity, income).
  Create them under [Accounting → Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).
- On a phone the sections run across the top.

**Edit** opens the same eight-step wizard used to create the product. Close
returns here. Share products **cannot be deleted** — there is no Delete.
Change a saved product with Edit.

**Dividends** opens [Share dividends](/help/products/share-dividends)
for this product (`?productId=`). That list declares and approves
payouts. The minimum-active-period field stays on Settings here.

## What a share product does not have

- **No start or close date.** Only [loan products](/help/products/loans/details)
  have a product availability window.
- **No accrual accounting.** Share accounting is None or Cash. Loan and savings
  products also offer Accrual (periodic); loans add Accrual (upfront).
- **No payment-channel mappings step.** Cash accounting still needs the four GL
  accounts on Accounting. Optional payment-channel and charge-to-income
  mappings are not in this wizard.

## Wizard steps

Create and edit walk the same steps. See
[Create a share product](/help/products/shares/create) for the fields and
rules.

1. Details — name, short name, description.
2. Currency — code, decimal places, multiples.
3. Terms — total shares, shares issued, unit price, and computed share capital.
4. Settings — min / nominal / max shares, lock-in, minimum active period for
   dividends, and dividends for inactive clients.
5. Market price — optional periods that set the share value from a date.
6. Charges — share charges in this product’s currency.
7. [Accounting](/help/products/shares/accounting) — None or Cash, and the GL
   accounts cash requires. A walkthrough creates **Normal Share With
   Accounting** and edits it.
8. Review — confirm every section, then create or save.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No products yet" | Use **New** to create the first product. If that button is missing, your role cannot create share products. |
| "No matching products" | Clear or change the search. |
| Product menu missing | Your role lacks share-product viewing permission. |
| Save rejected | The core banking system will refuse a field that does not match its rules. Read the message and fix the highlighted fields. |
| GL picker empty | Create a **detail** account of the matching type under [Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts). Header accounts never appear here. |

See the [products hub](/help/products) for how product types sit together.
