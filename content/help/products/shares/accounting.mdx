This is step 7 of the [create wizard](/help/products/shares/create). After
save, the product opens as a sectioned workspace. **Accounting** is on the
rail when the rule is Cash. Share products have **no** mappings step.

The walkthrough on this page creates **Normal Share With Accounting** on
**Cash**, opens the product, and edits the description and maximum shares.
Share products cannot be deleted. They have no start or close date, and **no
accrual** option.

This page explains the **accounting rule** and every **general ledger (GL)
account** cash needs. Create or adjust the accounts themselves under
[Accounting → Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).

On the seeded chart, example **codes** are in parentheses. Your institution
may use different codes; the **type** (asset, liability, equity, income)
must still match the picker.

## Accounting rule

Share products have two posting styles. There is **no** Accrual (periodic)
and **no** Accrual (upfront) — those exist on
[savings](/help/products/savings/accounting) and
[loan](/help/products/loans/accounting) products.

- **None** — no GL accounts. Use this while you are still designing the
  product, or when another system is the ledger. Share accounts can still
  be opened; they just do not write journal lines in Bankayo.
- **Cash** — a journal posts when share money **moves** (activation, a
  purchase, a redeem, a collected fee). Requires the four cash accounts
  below.

Every required account is a searchable list of **detail** GL accounts of
the matching type. **Header** accounts only group other accounts and never
appear here.

## Cash accounts

Required whenever the rule is Cash.

- **Share reference** (asset, `1110` Cash at Main Vault) — the cash or bank
  account share money moves through. Same idea as savings reference on a
  savings product, or fund source on a loan product.
- **Share suspense** (liability, `2130` Share Suspense) — money in flight
  between cash and equity, for example a purchase that has left cash and
  not yet landed in share equity.
- **Share equity** (equity, `3120` Share Equity) — subscribed capital.
  This picker lists **equity** accounts only.
- **Income from fees** (income, `4210` Income from Fees) — the product
  default for every share fee. Officers can still add a fee on the account
  if you did not attach charges on the product.

## Create, open, and edit

**New** on the [share products list](/help/products/shares) opens the
wizard. **Save** on Review creates the product and opens it: General, Terms,
and Settings, plus Fees and Accounting when Cash is on.

**Edit** reopens the same eight steps. Close returns to the product.
Share products **cannot be deleted** — unlike [savings](/help/products/savings)
products. Change a saved product with Edit.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Accrual is missing | Share products only offer None and Cash. Use a [savings](/help/products/savings/accounting) or [loan](/help/products/loans/accounting) product for accrual. |
| GL picker empty | Create a **detail** account of the matching type under [Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts). Equity accounts appear only on Share equity. |
| Save rejected on Cash | All four accounts are required. Fix the highlighted pickers. |
| No Delete | Share products have no delete in the core banking system. Edit the product instead. |
