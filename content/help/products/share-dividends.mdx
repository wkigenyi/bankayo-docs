---
title: Share dividends
description: Declare, approve, and delete share-product dividend payouts.
---

Open **Products → Share dividends** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"dividend"). You need **READ_DIVIDEND_SHAREPRODUCT**. Declare needs
**CREATE_DIVIDEND_SHAREPRODUCT**. Approve and delete need
**APPROVE_DIVIDEND_SHAREPRODUCT** and **DELETE_DIVIDEND_SHAREPRODUCT**.

This is Fineract `GET/POST /shareproduct/{productId}/dividend` and
`GET/PUT?command=approve/DELETE /shareproduct/{productId}/dividend/{id}`.

The [share product](/help/products/shares) wizard already has the
minimum-active-period field. This screen is the payout list — not that
setting.

## What you can do

- **Choose a share product.** The picker is `GET /products/share`. The
  URL keeps `?productId=` so a product detail **Dividends** link lands
  here.
- **Browse** initiated and approved payouts for that product. Status
  membership is Fineract `ShareProductDividendStatusType` (100
  Initiated, 300 Approved). There is no template `*Options` array; those
  two values are the enum (R8 fallback). Export CSV or Excel.
- **New dividend** opens a docked sidebar. Period start, period end
  (after start), and a positive amount are required
  (`ShareProductDataSerializer.validateDividendDetails`). Fineract
  refuses create when no eligible shares exist
  (`eligible.shares.not.found`).
- **Click a row** to see account allocations (`clientName`, account
  number, amount, status).
- **Approve** or **Delete** only while the payout is Initiated.
  Fineract refuses both once Approved.

Dates on the wire are `yyyy-MM-dd` with locale `en`. Amounts show the
ISO currency code from the selected product.
