title: Products

Open **Products** in the sidebar (a navigation group, not a page) or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "products". You only see the product types your role can
view.

Each product type has its own list, detail, and create/edit wizard. The **?**
button on a product screen opens the page for that type.

## Product types

- [Loan products](/help/products/loans) — the fullest wizard: terms, settings,
  charges, accounting, and mappings. Create and edit are eight steps.
- [Savings products](/help/products/savings) — eight-step wizard: interest
  compounding, posting, calculation, balances, charges, accounting, and
  mappings. Create and edit use the same steps. [Accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting)
  explains cash versus accrual, every GL account, and the terms (control,
  reference, suspense, interest payable, escheat, and the rest).
- [Share products](/help/products/shares) — eight-step wizard: total and
  issued shares, unit price, min / nominal / max, lock-in, dividends, market
  price, charges, and None or Cash accounting. Open a product for a
  sectioned workspace. Create and edit; unused products cannot be deleted.
  [Accounting](/help/products/shares/accounting) explains the four cash GL
  accounts.
- [Fixed deposits](/help/products/fixed) — term, amount, and an interest chart.
  Open a product for a sectioned workspace (general, terms, interest chart,
  accounting). Minimum and maximum term show as a length and unit together,
  for example 3 months.
- [Recurring deposits](/help/products/recurring) — like fixed deposits, with
  mandatory deposits and withdrawals. Detail uses the same sectioned layout.
- [Charges](/help/products/charges) — fees and penalties. Loan charges have
  five collection times and five calculations; attach them to a loan product
  or add them on a loan. Savings templates use the same catalog; add, pay,
  and waive them on [Savings charges](/help/savings/charges).
- [Collateral](/help/products/collaterals) — reusable types (name, quality,
  unit, base price, percent to base). Officers register quantity on a
  client, then pledge those units when opening a loan. This is not the
  loan-application collateral grid.
- [Products mix](/help/products/mix) — which loan products cannot be
  active together on the same client or group. Not the loan-product
  catalog.
- [Share dividends](/help/products/share-dividends) — declare and
  approve a payout for a share product. Not the minimum-active-period
  field on the share product itself.
- [Breach](/help/products/breach) — reusable working-capital breach
  rules. Not a loan-level breach action.
- [Near-breach](/help/products/near-breach) — reusable near-breach
  rules (threshold percent). Frequency units come from the breach
  template.

## Create and edit

**New** opens a full-page wizard. **Edit** uses the same wizard. Close on
create returns to the list; close on edit returns to the product you were
viewing. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). Amounts show the
currency **code**, not a symbol.

On a large screen, loan, savings, share, and charge wizards keep steps in a left rail. On a
tablet or phone the steps run across the top. **Previous** and **Next** (or
**Submit**) sit at the bottom of the page.

## Date ranges

Only [loan products](/help/products/loans/details) have an optional start and
close date (when the product may be used). Savings, share, charge, fixed
deposit, and recurring deposit products do **not** have a product availability
window in Fineract.

Fixed and recurring deposits do have **interest chart** dates (`from` /
`until`). Those belong to the rate schedule, not to the product itself.
