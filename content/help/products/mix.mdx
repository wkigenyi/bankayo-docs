---
title: Products mix
description: Loan products that cannot be active together on the same client or group.
---

Open **Products → Products mix** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "product mix").
You need **READ_PRODUCTMIX**. Create, update, and delete need the matching
`*_PRODUCTMIX` permission. Listing also uses `GET /loanproducts`, which
needs **READ_LOANPRODUCT**.

This is Fineract `GET /loanproducts?associations=productMixes`,
`GET /loanproducts/template?isProductMixTemplate=true`, and
`GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /loanproducts/{id}/productmix`.

This is **not** the [loan product](/help/products/loans) catalog. A mix
only records which other loan products Fineract will not let coexist
with this one. When an officer [opens a loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account),
`LoanApplicationValidator` refuses the application if the client or
group already has an active loan on a restricted product.

## What you can do

- **Browse** mixes by loan product name and the restricted products.
  Export CSV or Excel.
- **New mix** opens a docked sidebar. The product picker membership
  comes from `GET /loanproducts/template?isProductMixTemplate=true`
  `productOptions` (R8) — products that do not already have a mix.
  After you pick a product, restricted-product membership comes from
  `GET /loanproducts/{id}/productmix?template=true` (`allowedProducts`
  plus `restrictedProducts`). The product itself is omitted. Create
  requires at least one restricted product (`@NotEmpty`).
- **Click a row** to change the restricted set. The product cannot
  change. Update still requires at least one restricted product —
  use **Delete** to drop the mix.
- **Delete** removes every restriction for that product.

There is no enum `*Options` list on this resource. Product membership
is the template arrays above.
