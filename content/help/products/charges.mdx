Open **Products → Charges** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "charges").
You need charge-viewing permission to see the list, plus create, update, or
delete permission for those actions.

A charge is a **reusable template**: name, currency, when Fineract collects it,
how the amount is calculated, and whether it is a fee or a penalty. It is not
money by itself. A [loan product](/help/products/loans/charges) attaches the
templates that should start on every new loan. An officer can still add or
remove charges when [opening a loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account), and can
add more from the loan workspace **Charges** section.

This page covers **loan** charges in detail. Savings charges use the same
wizard with different times and calculations — see
[Other applies-to](#other-applies-to) and
[Savings charges](/help/savings/charges) for the account. Client, share, and
working-capital charges are summarised there too.

**New** and **Edit** use the same four-step wizard: applies to, terms, amount
and settings, and review. After create, **Applies to** cannot change —
Fineract does not allow it. Charges have **no** product availability window.

## The list

Each row is a template: name, applies to, collection time, amount, and active.

- **Search** filters as you type (name, applies-to, time, amount, active).
- **Export** downloads the catalog as CSV or Excel.
- **Click a row** to open the charge, then **Edit** for the wizard.
- Inactive charges stay in the catalog but do not appear when you attach
  charges to a new loan product.

## Creating — applies to

**New charge** opens the wizard. **Applies to** is the first step and the one
field you cannot change later.

| Applies to | Where it is used |
| --- | --- |
| **Loan** | Loan products and loan accounts. Five collection times, full calculation set, required payment mode. |
| Savings | Savings and deposit products. See [Savings charges](/help/savings/charges). |
| Client | Collected from the client, not a product. Flat, specified due date. Optional income account on the charge. |
| Shares | Share products. Cannot be a penalty. |
| Working capital | Working-capital loans. Specified due date, flat only. No tax group. |

Pick **Loan** for everything on this page.

## Collection time (loan)

**Charge time** is when Fineract puts the charge on the loan. Loan has five
values. Changing time resets calculation to the first allowed option and
clears due-day extras.

### Disbursement

Due when the loan is disbursed. Typical processing or origination fee.

**Cannot be a penalty.** The penalty switch is off and disabled.

### Specified due date

Due on a date the officer sets when they add the charge to an application or
to an existing loan. Use this for a one-off fee that is not tied to
disbursement or to every installment — insurance, a legal fee, a dated
penalty.

On the loan, **Add charge** requires a due date for this time type.

### Installment fee

Added to **each repayment installment** — a recurring processing fee, not a
late fee. It can be a fee or a penalty (penalty is unusual here).

### Overdue installment

A **late fee**. Fineract **requires** the penalty flag; Bankayo turns it on
and locks the switch.

Optional **add fee frequency** (days, weeks, months, or years, plus an
interval) controls how often the overdue charge repeats. Interval is required
when frequency is on.

### Tranche disbursement

For **multi-disburse** loans: collected when a tranche is disbursed, not only
on the first disbursement.

**Cannot be a penalty.** Calculation is only **flat** or **% of disbursement
amount**.

## Calculation (loan)

How the number is computed. The amount field is a currency amount for
**flat**, and a percent for the others.

| Calculation | Meaning | Caps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flat** | Fixed amount in the charge currency. | No |
| **% of amount** | Percent of principal (or of the relevant outstanding amount). | Optional min / max |
| **% of amount and interest** | Percent of principal plus interest. | Optional min / max |
| **% of interest** | Percent of interest only. | Optional min / max |
| **% of disbursement amount** | Percent of the disbursed amount. **Tranche disbursement only** — other times hide it. | Optional min / max |

Min and max caps are amounts in the charge currency. They bound the
*calculated* result so a percentage fee cannot fall below a floor or exceed a
ceiling. Both are optional; if both are set, min cannot be above max.

## Payment mode

Required on loan and working-capital charges.

- **Regular** — collected with the loan (repayment / cash path).
- **Account transfer** — collected by transfer from a savings account.

## Amount and settings

- **Amount** — the flat figure or the percent, depending on calculation.
- **Active** — defaults to on. Turn off to keep the template without offering
  it on new products.
- **Penalty** — marks the charge as a penalty rather than a fee. Locked off
  for disbursement and tranche disbursement; locked on for overdue
  installment.
- **Tax group** — optional when at least one group exists under
  [Organization → Tax groups](/help/organization#tax-groups). Create
  components first, then a group. Working-capital charges do not take a tax
  group.
- **Income account** — **not** on a loan charge. The product decides where a
  loan charge posts: a default on [accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting),
  overridden per charge on [mappings](/help/products/loans/mappings). See
  [Where a charge posts](#where-a-charge-posts). Client charges are the
  exception — they pick an income or liability account on the charge itself.

## Review, edit, delete

Review repeats applies to, terms, and amount. Save creates the template.

- **Edit** uses the same wizard. Applies to stays locked.
- **Delete** is refused if the charge is still attached to a product or a
  loan — read Fineract’s message.

Create [detail GL accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts) before a
product can map charge income.

## Attach to a loan product

On the product [Charges](/help/products/loans/charges) step, the table lists
loan charges in **this product’s currency**. Check a row to attach it. Search
and **All / Fee / Penalty** tabs filter the list.

Those attachments become the starting set when an officer opens a loan.
Officers can still add or remove charges on that loan. Fee and penalty income
GL accounts are configured on [mappings](/help/products/loans/mappings), not
on the charge.

If the product list is empty, create the charge here first, in the same
currency.

### Where a charge posts

Two steps on the product decide the GL account, and the second one wins.

1. **[Accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting)** sets the product
   defaults: one **income from fees** account for every fee on the product,
   and one **income from penalties** account for every penalty. Both are
   required once the rule is Cash or Accrual.
2. **[Mappings](/help/products/loans/mappings)** overrides that per charge.
   **Fee GL mappings** and **Penalty GL mappings** each take a row of
   *charge → income account*, so one fee or one penalty can post to its own
   preferred account while everything else keeps the default.

Add a row only when a charge needs its own account — an unmapped charge is
not an error, it just follows the product default. Mappings are optional,
appear only when accounting is not None, and can be changed later with
**Edit**. The accounts offered are the **detail** income accounts from
[Accounting → Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).

## On a loan

The loan workspace has a **Charges** section.

- The table shows name, fee or penalty, when it is collected, due date,
  amount, paid, outstanding, and status (outstanding, overdue, paid, waived).
- **Add charge** (needs loan-charge create permission) opens a docked
  sidebar. Pick a loan charge; specified due-date charges need a **due date**.
  Amount defaults from the template and can be changed.
- You cannot add charges on a closed, overpaid, or written-off loan.
- **Remove** (needs loan-charge delete permission) is only for a charge that
  has not been paid or waived and has no amount paid. Confirm in the dialog.

Product-attached charges also appear when [opening a loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account)
from the client — already selected; uncheck to drop one, or add another in
the same currency.

## On a savings account

Add, pay, waive, stop, and remove are documented under
[Savings charges](/help/savings/charges).

## Other applies-to

Same wizard, fewer combinations:

- **Savings** — activation, closure, withdrawal, annual, monthly, weekly,
  overdraft, no-activity, or specified due date. Flat, or percent of amount
  on withdrawal and no-activity. Annual and monthly fees need a due day;
  monthly and weekly need a repeat interval. Attach them on a
  [savings product](/help/products/savings/charges); manage them on the
  account under [Savings charges](/help/savings/charges). The product
  [accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting) step sets default income
  accounts, with optional per-charge mappings.
- **Client** — specified due date, flat only. Optional income account.
- **Shares** — activation (flat only), purchase, or redeem. Cannot be a
  penalty.
- **Working capital** — specified due date, flat, payment mode required, no
  tax group.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Charge missing on a loan product | Create it here in the **product currency**, keep it **active**, applies to **Loan**. |
| Penalty switch disabled | Disbursement and tranche cannot be penalties; overdue installment must be. |
| No percent of disbursement | That calculation exists only on tranche disbursement. |
| No min / max caps | Caps appear for percentage calculations on loan charges, not for flat. |
| No tax group field | Create a tax group under Organization first. |
| No income account on a loan charge | Map income on the loan product mappings step. |
| Cannot add a charge on a loan | The loan may be closed, overpaid, or written off, or no matching templates exist. |
| Cannot remove a loan charge | It is paid, waived, or already has an amount paid. |
| Cannot add, pay, waive, or remove a savings charge | See [Savings charges](/help/savings/charges). |
| Save rejected | Read the message from Fineract — applies-to cannot change after create. |
| Charge missing after search | Clear search; inactive charges still list here but not on a new product. |
