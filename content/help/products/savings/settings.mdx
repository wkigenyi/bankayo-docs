title: Savings product settings
description: Balances, lock-in, overdraft, lien, tax, and dormancy.

This is step 4 of the [create wizard](/help/products/savings/create). Extra
fields appear only when you turn a feature on. Money fields stay disabled
until you choose a [currency](/help/products/savings/currency).

Officers who [open a savings account](/help/clients#opening-an-account) see
these as defaults on Optional and can change them for that account.

## Balances

- **Minimum opening balance** — optional. The smallest amount that can
  activate an account on this product. Leave blank if an account may open
  with zero.
- **Minimum balance for interest** — optional. Interest is not calculated
  while the balance is below this floor. Use it so tiny balances do not earn
  (or cost you) interest. This is not the same as the minimum **required**
  balance below.
- **Minimum required balance** — optional. A floor the account should stay
  above after withdrawals.
- **Enforce minimum required balance** — when on, a withdrawal that would
  take the account below the minimum required balance is refused. When off,
  the minimum is informational.

## Lock-in period

Optional. The period and unit sit in one field, for example **6 months**.
Units come from the product template (days, weeks, months, or years).

During lock-in the client cannot withdraw (the core banking system enforces
this). Use it for compulsory or contractual savings. Leave the field
blank if funds may be withdrawn as soon as the account is active.

Clearing the period also clears the unit.

## Withdrawal fee on transfers

**Apply withdrawal fee to transfers** — whether a [withdrawal fee
charge](/help/products/savings/charges) also applies when funds move to
another account, not only when cash is taken out.

This does not create a fee by itself. Attach a withdrawal-fee charge on the
charges step, or the toggle has nothing to apply.

## Annual fee on the product

Optional **annual fee** amount. If you enter an amount, you must also choose
the **day** and **month** it falls due.

This is the product-level annual fee the core banking system still accepts
on the savings product itself. Prefer attaching an **annual fee**
[charge](/help/products/savings/charges) when you need the same fee on the
account’s Charges section, with pay / waive / stop. If you set both, you can
end up collecting two annual fees — use one approach.

## Overdraft

Turn **Allow overdraft** on if this product may go below zero.

Then you can set:

- **Overdraft limit** — how far below zero the account may go. Required if
  **Allow lien** is also on.
- **Overdraft interest rate** — nominal annual rate **charged** on the
  overdrawn amount (income to the institution). Separate from the
  [nominal annual interest rate](/help/products/savings/terms) paid on a
  positive balance.
- **Minimum overdraft for interest** — optional floor. Overdraft interest is
  not calculated until the overdrawn amount reaches this.

Even if you leave overdraft off, [cash or accrual accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting)
still needs the overdraft GL accounts (overdraft portfolio, income from
interest, write-off). Those sit unused until an account actually overdraws.

## Lien

A **lien** is a hold or claim on the account — typically so the institution
(or a linked loan) can block part of the balance.

Turn **Allow lien** on to set an optional **maximum lien limit**. If overdraft
and lien are both on, both limits are required and the overdraft limit
**cannot be greater than** the lien limit.

## Withhold tax

Turn **Withhold tax** on to deduct tax from interest when it is posted. You
must pick a [tax group](/help/organization#tax-groups). The picker is empty
until a group exists.

Tax withheld shows on the savings workspace when it applies. The tax group
defines the rate and the liability account the deduction posts to — not this
product’s interest-on-savings expense account.

## Dormancy

Turn **Track dormancy** on to age an account from last activity into
inactive, then dormant, then escheat.

You must enter three **day counts**, each **greater than** the last:

1. **Days to inactive** — no customer-initiated activity for this many days.
   The account can still show as inactive next to its usual status.
2. **Days to dormant** — longer still. Dormant accounts typically stop
   earning and may block withdrawals, depending on your institution’s
   process.
3. **Days to escheat** — unclaimed funds. Escheat moves the balance off the
   client’s savings control onto an **escheat liability** account (unclaimed
   property), so you no longer treat it as a live deposit.

When [accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting) is Cash or Accrual,
escheat also needs that **escheat liability** GL account. None does not.

The savings workspace shows these states next to status when they apply. See
[Savings](/help/savings).
