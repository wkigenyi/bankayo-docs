title: Review a savings product
description: Confirm every section, then create or save the product.
---

This is the last step of the [create wizard](/help/products/savings/create).
Each section repeats what you entered: details, currency, terms, settings,
charges, accounting, and mappings. Amounts use the currency **code**.

## Before you submit

- Jump back to a step from the rail if something looks wrong. Values stay
  filled in.
- Required extras for a feature you turned on (lock-in unit, annual-fee due
  day, tax group, dormancy days, GL accounts) must be complete or the wizard
  will not submit.
- If accounting is Cash or Accrual, check that **Savings reference** is a
  cash or bank **asset** and **Savings control** is the deposit **liability**.
  See [Accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting).
- The core banking system is the final check. A product that is already in
  use can be edited only in ways it allows; some combinations are rejected
  with a message from the core system.

## After save

You land on the [product workspace](/help/products/savings). Close without
saving returns to the list (create) or to the product (edit).

Officers can now [open savings accounts](/help/clients#opening-an-account)
on this product. Journals for those accounts use the GL accounts you set,
when accounting is not None. You can inspect postings under
[Journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries).
