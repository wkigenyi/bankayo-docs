Open **Products → Savings** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "savings
products"). You need permission to view savings products — if the menu item is
missing, ask your administrator.

A savings product is the template for every savings account opened on it. It
sets the currency, the advertised interest rate, how interest compounds and
posts, optional opening and lock-in rules, overdraft and lien, tax and
dormancy, attached charges, and — when books are kept in Bankayo — the
general-ledger accounts those movements post to.

When an officer [opens a savings account](/help/clients#opening-an-account),
the docked sidebar starts from this product. **Required** is the product and
dates; **Optional** can still change interest, balances, lock-in, overdraft,
lien, tax, and charges for that one account.

## The list

Each row is a product: name, short name, currency, and accounting rule.

- **Search** with the box above the table.
- **New** starts the [create wizard](/help/products/savings/create) when your
  role allows it.
- **Open** a product by clicking its row.

## Opening a product

The product opens as a workspace with a section rail — the same pattern as a
loan product.

- **General**, **Terms**, and **Settings** are always there.
- **Fees**, **Penalties**, **Accounting**, channel mappings, and fee/penalty
  GL mappings appear only when the product has them.
- Charge names link to the [charge](/help/products/charges).
- Accounting lists GL accounts by type (assets, income, expenses, liabilities).
  Create them under [Accounting → Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).
- On a phone the sections run across the top.

**Edit** opens the same eight-step wizard used to create the product. Close
returns here.

## What a savings product does not have

- **No start or close date.** Only [loan products](/help/products/loans/details)
  have a product availability window.
- **No upfront accrual.** Savings accounting is None, Cash, or Accrual
  (periodic). Loan products also offer Accrual (upfront).
- **No fund label.** Loan products can name an [organization fund](/help/organization/funds).
  Savings products do not.
- **No override checkboxes.** Loan products lock some terms unless you allow
  overrides. On savings, the open-account sidebar always offers the product
  defaults as optional changes for that account.

## Wizard steps

Create and edit walk the same steps. Each has its own help page. Two
videos cover the wizard: [create](/help/products/savings/create) (None,
unsaved) and
[cash accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting) (Cash, saved as
**Normal Savings With Accounting**).

1. [Details](/help/products/savings/details) — name, short name, description.
2. [Currency](/help/products/savings/currency) — code, decimal places,
   multiples.
3. [Terms](/help/products/savings/terms) — nominal annual rate, compounding,
   posting, calculation, and days in year.
4. [Settings](/help/products/savings/settings) — balances, lock-in, overdraft,
   lien, tax, and dormancy.
5. [Charges](/help/products/savings/charges) — fees and penalties in this
   product’s currency.
6. [Accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting) — None, cash, or accrual
   periodic, every GL account that rule needs, and what the terms mean.
7. [Mappings](/help/products/savings/mappings) — payment channels and charges
   to GL accounts (when accounting is not None).
8. [Review](/help/products/savings/review) — confirm every section, then
   create or save.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No products yet" | Use **New** to create the first product. If that button is missing, your role cannot create savings products. |
| "No matching products" | Clear or change the search. |
| Product menu missing | Your role lacks savings-product viewing permission. |
| Save rejected | The core banking system will refuse a product that is still used, or a field that does not match its rules. Read the message and fix the highlighted fields. |
| GL picker empty | Create a **detail** account of the matching type under [Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts). Header accounts never appear here. |
| Savings reference picker has no "Savings Reference" | That seeded name is a **liability**. The picker only lists **asset** (cash or bank) accounts. See [Accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting). |
