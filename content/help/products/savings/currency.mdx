This is step 2 of the [create wizard](/help/products/savings/create).

## Choosing a currency

Currency is a searchable list of currencies your institution has **enabled**
under [Organization → Currencies](/help/organization#currencies). Each option
shows the name and the **code** (for example `KES`, not a symbol).

Choosing a currency fills **decimal places** from that currency. You can still
change decimal places on the product if the core banking system allows it
(0–6). Decimal places control how money fields round on later steps and on
accounts opened on this product.

If the currency you need is missing, enable it under Currencies first, then
return here.

## Multiples

**In multiples of** is optional. Opening balances, limits, and similar
amounts round to this step (for example 1, 10, or 100). Leave it blank if any
amount in this currency is allowed.

## Amounts on later steps

Fields such as minimum opening balance stay **disabled** until a currency is
chosen. From then on they show the currency **code** in the field — never `$`,
`€`, or `KSh`.

[Charges](/help/products/savings/charges) are filtered to this currency.
Change currency after attaching charges and unmatched charges disappear from
the list. Accounting GL accounts are not currency-specific in the picker —
use accounts your institution keeps in this product’s currency.
