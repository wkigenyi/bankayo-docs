title: Savings product charges
description: Attach fees and penalties in the product currency.

This is step 5 of the [create wizard](/help/products/savings/create). Define
the templates first under [Charges](/help/products/charges) — collection
time and calculation are on the charge, not here. After save, attached fees
and penalties show as tables on the product when any exist. Charge names
link to that catalog.

## What you can attach

The table lists charges that apply to **savings** and match this product’s
[currency](/help/products/savings/currency). Search and the **All / Fee /
Penalty** tabs filter the list. Check a row to attach that charge; uncheck
to remove it from the product.

Product charges become the starting set when an officer
[opens a savings account](/help/clients#opening-an-account) — they can still
add or remove charges on that account, and manage them later from
[Savings charges](/help/savings/charges) (pay, waive, stop recurring, or
remove while pending).

If the list is empty, either no savings charges exist in this currency, or
you have not chosen a currency yet. Create the charge first under
[Charges](/help/products/charges), in the same currency as the product.

## Savings collection times

A savings charge is collected at one of these times (set on the charge
template):

- **Specified due date** — you (or the officer) pick the due date.
- **Savings activation** — when the account is activated.
- **Savings closure** — when the account is closed.
- **Withdrawal fee** — when the client withdraws. Pair with
  [Apply withdrawal fee to transfers](/help/products/savings/settings) if
  account-to-account transfers should pay it too.
- **Annual fee** / **Monthly fee** / **Weekly fee** — recurring. Annual and
  monthly need a due day; monthly and weekly need a repeat interval.
- **Overdraft fee** — when the account is overdrawn.
- **No-activity fee** — when the account has no qualifying activity.

Calculation is **flat**, or **percent of amount** on withdrawal and
no-activity fees.

## Product annual fee versus a charge

[Settings](/help/products/savings/settings) still accept a product-level
annual fee amount and due day. That is separate from attaching an annual-fee
**charge** here. Use charges when you want the fee on the account Charges
section. Do not set both unless you intend two fees.

## After save

You can change the attached set later with **Edit**. When accounting is not
None, you can send one fee or penalty to its own income account on
[mappings](/help/products/savings/mappings); charges with no row keep the
product default income accounts.
