title: Create a savings product
description: The eight-step wizard for creating or editing a savings product.
---

**New** on the [savings products list](/help/products/savings) opens this
wizard. **Edit** on a product uses the same steps and returns to that product
when you close.

On a large screen, steps stay in a left rail. **Previous** and **Next** sit at
the bottom of the page. On a tablet or phone the steps run across the top.
Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). Amounts show the currency
**code**, not a symbol.

You can jump back to an earlier step without losing what you typed. Bankayo
only asks for extra fields when you turn a feature on — for example overdraft
limits after you allow overdraft, a tax group after withhold tax, three
dormancy day counts after you track dormancy, GL accounts after you leave
accounting on None.

The video on this page walks the wizard and stops on **None** without
saving. A second walkthrough,
[savings product with cash accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting),
creates **Normal Savings With Accounting**, sets **Cash**, maps a payment
channel, and saves.

## The eight steps

1. [Details](/help/products/savings/details) — name, short name, description.
2. [Currency](/help/products/savings/currency) — code, decimal places,
   multiples. Amounts stay disabled until a currency is chosen.
3. [Terms](/help/products/savings/terms) — nominal annual rate, compounding,
   posting, calculation, and days in year.
4. [Settings](/help/products/savings/settings) — balances, lock-in, overdraft,
   lien, tax, and dormancy.
5. [Charges](/help/products/savings/charges) — fees and penalties in this
   product’s currency.
6. [Accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting) — None, cash, or accrual
   periodic, and the GL accounts that rule requires.
7. [Mappings](/help/products/savings/mappings) — payment channels and charges
   to GL accounts (when accounting is not None).
8. [Review](/help/products/savings/review) — confirm every section, then
   create or save.

## Rules that span steps

The core banking system will reject combinations that do not work together.
Bankayo blocks the common ones in the form:

- A **lock-in period** needs both a length and a unit (days, weeks, months,
  or years).
- An **annual fee** on settings needs both an amount and a due day.
- **Withhold tax** needs a [tax group](/help/organization#tax-groups).
- **Dormancy** needs three increasing day counts (inactive, then dormant,
  then escheat).
- If **lien** and **overdraft** are both on, both limits are required and the
  overdraft limit cannot be greater than the lien limit.
- **Cash or accrual** accounting needs the matching GL accounts; **None**
  does not. Savings accounting has no upfront accrual option. Overdraft GL
  accounts are required on cash and accrual even if the product does not
  allow overdraft. Tracking dormancy with books on also needs an escheat
  liability account.

If save still fails, the message comes from the core banking system — fix
the highlighted fields and try again.

Creating needs savings-product create permission; editing needs update
permission. Viewing a product you cannot edit still opens the definition
without **Edit**.
