title: Review a loan product
description: Confirm every section, then create or save the product.
---

This is the last step of the [create wizard](/help/products/loans/create).
Each section repeats what you entered: details, currency, terms, settings,
charges, accounting, and mappings. Amounts use the currency **code**.

## Before you submit

- Jump back to a step from the rail if something looks wrong. Values stay
  filled in.
- Required extras for a feature you turned on (tranche count, guarantee
  percentages, GL accounts, payment allocation) must be complete or the
  wizard will not submit.
- Fineract is the final check. A product that is already in use can be
  edited only in ways Fineract allows; some combinations are rejected with a
  message from the core system.

## After save

You land on the [product workspace](/help/products/loans). Close without
saving returns to the list (create) or to the product (edit). The
[cash-accounting walkthrough](/help/products/loans/accounting) clicks
**Save** and opens that workspace.

Officers can now [open loans](/help/clients#opening-an-account) on this
product, within the ranges and overrides you set.
