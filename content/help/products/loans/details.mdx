title: Loan product details
description: Name, short name, optional dates, fund, and borrower cycle.
---

This is step 1 of the [create wizard](/help/products/loans/create). After
save, the same fields appear under **General** on the product.

## Identity

- **Name** — up to 100 characters. This is what officers see when they open a
  loan.
- **Short name** — at most **four** characters. Fineract uses it as a compact
  code; keep it unique enough to recognise on lists.
- **Description** — optional, up to 500 characters.
- **External ID** — optional reference from another system, up to 100
  characters.
- **Fund** — optional. This is the donor, programme, or internal pool this
  product belongs to — a label, not a ledger account. The list comes from
  [Organization → Funds](/help/organization/funds). The field is hidden until
  at least one fund exists. After save it appears under **General** on the
  product. Officers still pick a fund when they
  [open a loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account); Bankayo copies this
  value onto the application as the default. Leave it blank if the product
  is not tied to one source of capital (**No fund**). The
  [cash-accounting walkthrough](/help/products/loans/accounting) sets
  **Internal**. Do not confuse this with **fund source** on
  [accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting), which is a GL cash or bank
  account.

## When the product may be used

**Start date** and **close date** are optional. They are the product
availability window — the only product type in Bankayo that has one.

- Leave both blank if the product should stay available.
- If you set both, close date cannot be before start date.
- Savings, share, charge, and deposit products have no equivalent dates.

## Borrower cycle

- **Include in borrower cycle** — count loans on this product toward the
  client’s cycle number.
- **Use borrower cycle** — vary principal, interest rate, or number of
  repayments by how many loans the client has already taken.

When **Use borrower cycle** is on, you add rows for principal, interest, and
repayments. Each row is a cycle number with a condition (equal to, or
greater than), a default value, and optional min/max. Rows must run in
sequence — Fineract rejects a gap or an out-of-order condition.

Turn the toggle off and the variation tables are cleared.
