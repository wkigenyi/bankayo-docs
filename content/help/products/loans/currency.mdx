This is step 2 of the [create wizard](/help/products/loans/create).

## Choosing a currency

Currency is a searchable list of currencies your institution has **enabled**
under [Organization → Currencies](/help/organization#currencies). Each option
shows the name and the **code** (for example `KES`, not a symbol).

Choosing a currency fills **decimal places** from that currency. You can still
change decimal places on the product if Fineract allows it (0–6).

If the currency you need is missing, enable it under Currencies first, then
return here.

## Multiples

- **In multiples of** — optional. Principal and similar amounts round to this
  step (for example 1, 10, or 100).
- **Installment amount in multiples of** — optional. Used when the product
  lets officers set an installment amount.

## Amounts on later steps

Fields such as principal stay **disabled** until a currency is chosen. From
then on they show the currency **code** in the field — never `$`, `€`, or
`KSh`.

[Charges](/help/products/loans/charges) on the next-but-one step are filtered
to this currency. Change currency after attaching charges and unmatched
charges disappear from the list.
