Open **Products → Loan products** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "loan
products"). You need permission to view loan products — if the menu item is
missing, ask your administrator.

## The list

Each row is a product: name, short name, currency, and accounting rule.

- **Search** with the box above the table.
- **New** starts the [create wizard](/help/products/loans/create) when your
  role allows it.
- **Open** a product by clicking its row.

## Opening a product

The product opens as a workspace with a section rail — the same pattern as a
client record.

- **General**, **Terms**, and **Settings** are always there.
- **Fees**, **Penalties**, **Accounting**, channel mappings, fee/penalty
  GL mappings, and write-off or charge-off reason mappings appear only when
  the product has them.
- Charge names link to the [charge](/help/products/charges).
- Accounting lists GL accounts by type (assets, income, expenses, liabilities).
  Create them under [Accounting → Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).
- On a phone the sections run across the top.

**Edit** opens the same eight-step wizard used to create the product. Close
returns here.

## What a loan product controls

A loan product is the template for every loan opened on it: currency, principal
and interest ranges, repayment pattern, optional features (multi-disburse,
top-up, down payment, guarantees, interest recalculation), attached charges,
and how the loan posts to the books.

When a loan officer [opens a loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account), Bankayo
fills those defaults. Fields the product marks as overridable can still be
changed on that loan; the rest stay locked.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No products yet" | Use **New** to create the first product. If that button is missing, your role cannot create loan products. |
| "No matching products" | Clear or change the search. |
| Product menu missing | Your role lacks loan-product viewing permission. |
| Save rejected | Fineract will refuse a product that is still used, or a field that does not match its rules. Read the message and fix the highlighted fields. |
