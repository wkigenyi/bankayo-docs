**New** on the [loan products list](/help/products/loans) opens this wizard.
**Edit** on a product uses the same steps and returns to that product when you
close.

On a large screen, steps stay in a left rail. **Previous** and **Next** sit at
the bottom of the page. On a tablet or phone the steps run across the top. Required fields
are marked with an asterisk (*).

You can jump back to an earlier step without losing what you typed. Bankayo
only asks for extra fields when you turn a feature on — for example tranche
count appears after multi-disburse, GL accounts after you leave accounting
on None.

The video on this page walks the wizard and stops on **None** without
saving. A second walkthrough,
[loan product with cash accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting),
creates **Normal Loan With Accounting**, sets **Cash**, maps a payment
channel, and saves.

## The eight steps

1. [Details](/help/products/loans/details) — name, short name, optional dates,
   fund, borrower cycle.
2. [Currency](/help/products/loans/currency) — code, decimal places, multiples.
   Amounts stay disabled until a currency is chosen.
3. [Terms](/help/products/loans/terms) — principal, repayments, fixed or
   floating interest.
4. [Settings](/help/products/loans/settings) — schedule, grace, and optional
   features (multi-disburse, top-up, guarantees, and so on).
5. [Charges](/help/products/loans/charges) — fees and penalties in this
   product’s currency, with amount, collection, calculation, and related
   attributes.
6. [Accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting) — None, cash, or accrual, and
   the GL accounts that rule requires.
7. [Mappings](/help/products/loans/mappings) — payment channels, charges, and
   write-off or charge-off reasons to GL accounts (when accounting is not
   None).
8. [Review](/help/products/loans/review) — confirm every section, then create
   or save.

## Rules that span steps

Fineract will reject combinations that do not work together. Bankayo blocks
the common ones in the form:

- A **floating rate** needs declining-balance interest and interest
  recalculation.
- A **progressive** schedule needs the advanced payment allocation strategy.
- **Cash or accrual** accounting needs the matching GL accounts; **None**
  does not.
- Optional features only show the extra fields Fineract requires when that
  feature is on.

If save still fails, the message comes from Fineract — fix the highlighted
fields and try again.

## Permissions

Creating needs loan-product create permission; editing needs update
permission. Viewing a product you cannot edit still opens the workspace
without **Edit**.
