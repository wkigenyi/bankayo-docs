---
title: Loan product charges
description: Attach fees and penalties in the product currency.
---

This is step 5 of the [create wizard](/help/products/loans/create). Define the
templates first under [Charges](/help/products/charges) — collection time,
calculation, payment mode, and penalty rules are on the charge, not here.
After save, attached fees and penalties show as tables on the product when any
exist. Charge names link to that catalog.

## What you can attach

The table lists charges that apply to loans **and** match this product’s
[currency](/help/products/loans/currency). Each row keeps the details on
three columns so you can scan without scrolling sideways:

- **Charge** — name, fee or penalty, and (when inactive) that status. Secondary
  text is when it is collected, how it is calculated, payment mode, and any
  due day or frequency.
- **Amount** — currency amount or percent, with min/max caps underneath when
  they exist.
- **Ledger** — income account, with the tax group underneath when one is set.

Search and the **All / Fee / Penalty** tabs filter the list. Check a row (or
click it) to attach that charge; uncheck to remove it from the product.
Product charges become the starting set when an officer
[opens a loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account) — they can still add or
remove charges on that loan.

If the list is empty, either no loan charges exist in this currency, or you
have not chosen a currency yet. Create the charge first under
[Charges](/help/products/charges), in the same currency as the product.

## Fees versus penalties

On the product workspace they split into **Fees** and **Penalties** sections.
Overdue installment charges are penalties; disbursement and tranche charges
cannot be.

## After save

You can change the attached set later with **Edit**. Accounting mappings for
individual fees and penalties are configured on the
[mappings](/help/products/loans/mappings) step when books are not None.
