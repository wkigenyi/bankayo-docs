---
title: Loan product terms
description: Principal, repayments, and fixed or floating interest.
---

This is step 3 of the [create wizard](/help/products/loans/create). Related
fields share a row: min / default / max principal, min / default / max
repayments, and the same pattern for interest.

## Principal

Enter a **default** principal, and optionally a **minimum** and **maximum**.
The default must sit inside that range. Officers who open a loan see this
default and the allowed range; they can only change the amount if
[settings](/help/products/loans/settings) allow the matching override.

## Repayments

- **Number of repayments** — default, with optional min and max.
- **Repay every** — the interval and unit together, for example every
  1 month, or every 2 weeks.
- **Repayment start** — from disbursement or from the submitted date,
  depending on the options Fineract offers.

Grace periods on the settings step cannot be as long as (or longer than) the
number of repayments.

## Interest — fixed

Leave **floating rate** off to set a nominal **interest rate per period**,
with optional min/max, and an **interest rate frequency** (per month, year,
and so on). Officers who open a loan see that rate and the allowed range.

## Interest — floating

Turn **floating rate** on when the product should track a published floating
rate plus a differential.

Fineract requires all of the following; Bankayo will not let you continue
without them:

- Interest type **declining balance** (set on [settings](/help/products/loans/settings)).
- **Interest recalculation** enabled (also on settings).
- A **floating rate** from
  [Organization → Floating rates](/help/organization#floating-rates). The
  field stays empty until at least one active rate exists.
- A differential, plus min / default / max lending differentials that stay in
  order.

Equal amortization cannot be combined with a floating rate.

On the client loan wizard, officers set the **differential** (inside the
product min / default / max) instead of a nominal rate. If the product allows
floating calculation, they can also choose whether this loan **tracks later
published rate changes**. Create the published rate under
[Organization → Floating rates](/help/organization#floating-rates) first.
