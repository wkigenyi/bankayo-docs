---
title: Loan product mappings
description: Map payment channels, charges, and reasons to GL accounts.
---

This is step 7 of the [create wizard](/help/products/loans/create). Mappings
are optional. They appear only when [accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting)
is Cash or Accrual. If the rule is None, this step explains that and you can
continue to review.

The cash-accounting walkthrough maps the institution payment type **Cash**
to fund-source GL `1220`. Fee and penalty rows only appear when the product
has matching charges attached.

Each mapping is a row: something officers pick (a payment type, a charge, or
a reason) and the general ledger account it should post to. Add a row, choose
both sides, or remove a row you do not need.

The charge rows are **overrides**. [Accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting)
already sets one income account for every fee on the product and one for every
penalty; a row here sends a single [charge](/help/products/charges) somewhere
else instead.

## What you can map

- **Payment channels** to fund-source GL accounts (payment type → account).
  The list is institution payment types from
  [Organization → Payment types](/help/organization#payment-types) — not
  Fineract’s system-generated types (transfers, down payment, and similar).
  Those exist for other loan operations, not cash channels. The mapping
  table is hidden until at least one institution type exists.
- **Fees** to income accounts — this **overrides** the product's default
  *income from fees* account for that one charge. Charges with no row keep the
  default.
- **Penalties** to income accounts — the same override against *income from
  penalties*.
- **Charge-off reasons** to expense accounts.
- **Write-off reasons** to expense accounts.

Reason lists come from
[Organization → Loan reasons](/help/organization#loan-reasons). Each mapping
table is hidden until at least one reason and one expense account exist.
These mappings show as their own sections on the product after save.

## After save

You can change mappings later with **Edit**. GL accounts themselves are
maintained under
[Accounting → Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).
