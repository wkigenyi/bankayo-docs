A share account has its own workspace — not the client header or section list.
Open one from a [client’s Shares section](/help/clients#client-details). The
product that account was opened on is defined under
[Share products](/help/products/shares).

The top of the page keeps the account number, status, account type (with an
icon), product, a link to the client, and approved shares, then splits into
sections:

- **Overview** — holdings (approved shares, pending approval, market price and
  value, linked savings) next to product, requested shares, and important dates.
  Amounts use the currency **code**.
- **Transactions** — purchases and redemptions, 25 to a page (you can choose 10,
  50, or 100). Filter by transaction date and type; the address bar keeps those
  filters. Reversed postings are hidden until you turn on **Show reversed**.
  Pending additional purchases can be approved or rejected from the row menu.
- **Charges** — fees on this account (read-only for now).

**Actions** depend on status and your permissions. Commands that need a date or
share count open a docked sidebar over the account — the header stays visible.
Confirm-only commands (undo approval, approve or reject a pending purchase) ask
you to confirm.

Typical work from the share account header:

- **Pending** — approve or reject the application.
- **Approved** — activate, or undo approval.
- **Active** — apply for additional shares, redeem shares, or close the account.
  When the account is active and has approved shares, **Download certificate**
  saves a PDF of share ownership (member, account, approved shares, market
  price, and signature lines). Amounts use the currency **code**.

From a client list, clicking a share row opens this same workspace.

**Import shares** (Institution) bulk-creates share accounts or purchases
additional shares from a spreadsheet. See [Import shares](/help/shares/import).

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Open a share account from a client" | Click a share account on a client. |
| Account missing or not loading | It may have been closed — tell your administrator. |
| No Download certificate | The account must be active and have approved shares. |
