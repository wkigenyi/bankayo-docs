---
title: Fixed deposit statement
description: Preview a date range of fixed deposit postings and download a PDF.
---

The fixed deposit workspace has a **Statement** section. Open it from an
account — there is no institution-wide statement list. The **?** button on that
section opens this page.

Fineract does not expose a statement API. Bankayo builds the statement from the
same postings already loaded on the account: reversed rows are omitted, remaining
rows are ordered oldest first, and opening balance is the running balance of the
last posting strictly before **From**. Closing balance is the last running balance
in the range, or the opening balance when the range is empty.

Credits are deposits, interest postings, amount releases, dividend payouts, and
inward account transfers. Debits are withdrawals, fee deductions, overdraft
interest, tax withheld, amount holds, escheat, write-offs, and outward account
transfers. Office-transfer status postings are not treated as money in or out.
When Fineract sent a transfer description, that text is used instead of the
plain type.

Amounts use the currency **code**, never a symbol. Download PDF asks the server
for a file (`statement_{account}_{from}_{to}.pdf`). You need permission to read
the fixed deposit account.

## Date range

The page opens on the last calendar month through today. Change **From** and
**To**, then **Show statement**. The preview table and the PDF use that applied
range. **From** cannot be after **To**.

## Cash receipts

A cash deposit or withdrawal that is not reversed and not an account transfer
can download a receipt PDF from the transaction row. After you record a cash
deposit or withdrawal from the header, the sidebar stays open with **Download
receipt** when Fineract returned the new transaction id. Account transfers do
not get a receipt.

See [Fixed deposits](/help/fixed) for the rest of the workspace. Recurring
deposits use the same statement and receipt pattern on
[Recurring deposits](/help/recurring/statement).
