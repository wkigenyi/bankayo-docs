## September 2026

### What customizing Fineract includes

Bankayo customizes Apache Fineract for the institution: the operator UI, the
backend around the core, integrations, and compliance — internal controls and
what the bodies that regulate you require — not only a new front end. See
[Fineract help, UI, and customization](/help/fineract).

### Documentation on sign-in

The sign-in screen links to **[docs.bankayo.io](https://docs.bankayo.io/help/signing-in)**
for help signing in. See [Signing in](/help/signing-in).

### Dashboard figures

The home **Dashboard** shows live portfolio figures for the office you select:
clients, loan outstanding and overdue (including PAR 30), pending approval and
disbursal, disbursements, expected collections, savings, checker inbox, offices,
and staff. Tiles open the matching screen. **[Portfolio health](/help/portfolio-health)**
adds PAR 60/90, product mix, and days-past-due bands. PAR 30 is loans **30 or
more** days past due, not every loan in arrears. Figures stay on the server.
See [Finding your way around](/help/navigation).

### Income statement and balance sheet

**Income statement** and **Balance sheet** are first-class screens (sidebar and
Ctrl+K), not only catalog rows. They look up the published table reports
**Income Statement Table** and **Balance Sheet Table**, run the current period
plus the same window one and two years earlier, and download a PDF. Year-over-year
columns are extra runs of the same Fineract report. On the balance sheet,
Retained Earnings (`3XXX`) is a presentation plug so the statement closes — not
a general-ledger posting. Opening those two names from the catalog redirects
here. Trial Balance stays on the generic runner. See
[Income statement](/help/reports/income-statement) and
[Balance sheet](/help/reports/balance-sheet).

## August 2026

### Import shares

**Import shares** (Institution) bulk-creates share accounts or purchases
additional shares from an Excel spreadsheet. Match clients by external ID,
preview rows, and post valid purchases one at a time against a selected share
product. This replaces the Fineract share-account workbook under
Organization bulk import. See [Import shares](/help/shares/import).

### Import savings

**Import savings** (Institution) bulk-posts cash deposits from an Excel
spreadsheet (`POST /savingsaccounts/{id}/transactions?command=deposit`).
Match clients by external ID, preview rows, and post valid deposits one at
a time. This replaces the Fineract savings-account workbook under
Organization bulk import. See [Import savings](/help/savings/import).

### Bulk import navigation

Each bulk-import type is its own Organization screen (**Import offices**,
**Import clients**, **Import loans**, and the rest). Open them from the
sidebar or Ctrl+K — there is no catalog item in navigation. Journal entries
stay under Accounting →
[Import journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-import) — not the Fineract
workbook hub. Savings deposits stay under Institution →
[Import savings](/help/savings/import). Share purchases stay under Institution →
[Import shares](/help/shares/import). See [Bulk import](/help/organization/bulk-import).

### Near-breach

**Near-breach** (Products) lists reusable working-capital near-breach
rules (`GET/POST /working-capital/near-breach`). Frequency membership
comes from the breach template — there is no near-breach template API.
This is not a loan-level near-breach action. See
[Near-breach](/help/products/near-breach).

### Breach

**Breach** (Products) lists reusable working-capital breach rules
(`GET/POST /working-capital/breach/breaches`). Frequency and amount
calculation membership come from `GET /working-capital/breach/template`.
This is not a loan-level breach action. See
[Breach](/help/products/breach).

### Share dividends

**Share dividends** (Products) lists payouts for a share product
(`GET/POST /shareproduct/{id}/dividend`). Declare a period and amount,
then approve or delete while the payout is still initiated. Status is
Fineract `ShareProductDividendStatusType` (initiated / approved). This
is not the minimum-active-period field on the share product. See
[Share dividends](/help/products/share-dividends).

### Products mix

**Products mix** (Products) lists loan products that cannot be active
together (`GET /loanproducts?associations=productMixes`). Product
membership comes from the mix template. This is not the loan-product
catalog. See [Products mix](/help/products/mix).

### Collateral catalog

**Collateral** (Products) lists reusable collateral types
(`GET/POST /collateral-management`). Currency membership comes from
`GET /collateral-management/template`, kept to enabled organisation
currencies. This is not the loan-application collateral grid. See
[Collateral](/help/products/collaterals).

### Loan originators

**Loan originators** (Organization) lists parties that originate loans
(`GET/POST /loan-originators`). Status and type membership come from
`GET /loan-originators/template`. Attach/detach stays on the loan. See
[Loan originators](/help/organization/loan-originators).

### Investors

**Investors** (Organization) lists external asset-owner transfers
(`POST /external-asset-owners/search`). Cancel the latest pending or
buyback transfer. Sale and buyback stay on the loan. See
[Investors](/help/organization/investors).

### Fund mapping

**Fund mapping** (Organization) searches outstanding loans by office and
product (`POST /search/advance`). Product and office membership comes
from `GET /search/template`. This is not the Funds catalog and not a
fund-to-GL map. See [Fund mapping](/help/organization/fund-mapping).

### Ad-hoc query

**Ad-hoc query** (Organization) lists saved SQL the
**Generate AdhocClient Schedule** job inserts into a table. Frequency
membership comes from `GET /adhocquery/template`. This is not a live
SQL console and not Reports. See
[Ad-hoc query](/help/organization/adhoc-query).

### Entity data-table checks

**Entity data-table checks** (Organization) require a registered extra-data
table when a client, group, loan, or savings account reaches a status.
Entity and status membership come from `GET /entityDatatableChecks/template`.
Fineract has no update API. This is not System → Manage data tables. See
[Entity data-table checks](/help/organization/entity-data-table-checks).

### Password preferences

**Password preferences** (Organization) chooses the seeded Fineract
policy for new user passwords (`simple`, `secure`, `strong`). Membership
comes from `GET /passwordpreferences/template`. See
[Password preferences](/help/organization/password-preferences).

### Bulk loan reassignment

**Bulk loan reassignment** (Organization) moves selected loans from one
loan officer to another at the same office. Offices and officers come
from `GET /loans/loanreassignment/template`. See
[Bulk loan reassignment](/help/organization/bulk-loan-reassignment).

### Bulk import

**Bulk import** (Organization) is Fineract Excel templates for offices,
clients, loans, and other entities. Each type is a sidebar link
(**Import offices**, **Import clients**, and the rest). Download a
template, fill it, and upload it. Previous imports show row counts and an
output workbook for errors. This is not the journal preview importer or
the groups-list shortcut. See
[Bulk import](/help/organization/bulk-import).

### SMS campaigns

**SMS campaigns** (Organization) lists Fineract SMS and notification
campaigns. **New campaign** is a four-step wizard (details, audience,
message, review). Activate, close, or reactivate from the campaign page.
Sending needs an SMS gateway; the UI still talks to `/smscampaigns`. See
[SMS campaigns](/help/organization/sms-campaigns).

### Credit bureau

**Credit bureau** (Institution) maps a seeded Fineract bureau to this
institution, attaches loan products, and stores connection keys. It is not
a client credit profile and not the CB-ILD plugin. See
[Credit bureau](/help/credit-bureau).

### Account transfers and standing instructions

**Account transfers** (Institution) lists one-off postings between savings
accounts or from savings to a loan. **New transfer** is a four-step wizard
(from, to, details, review). Open a transfer to undo it.
**Standing instructions** schedules those transfers. **New instruction** is
a five-step wizard; edit cannot change from/to accounts or the name.
**Standing instruction history** (Organization) lists each run. These are
not repay-from-savings on a loan workspace. See
[Account transfers](/help/account-transfers),
[Standing instructions](/help/standing-instructions), and
[Standing instruction history](/help/organization/standing-instructions-history).

### Collection sheet

**Collection sheet** (Institution) finds centers with a collection meeting
on a date for an office and staff member. Open a center to post attendance
and repayments on that center's sheet. **Individual collection sheet** posts
bulk repayments and mandatory savings for individual loans in an office.
These are not the sheet on a group or center workspace. See
[Collection sheet](/help/collection-sheet) and
[Individual collection sheet](/help/individual-collection-sheet).

### Account number preferences

**System → Account number preferences** lists Fineract account number formats
(one per entity: client, loan, savings, center, group, shares, working capital
loan). Create, edit, or delete a prefix; membership comes from
`GET /accountnumberformats/template`. Sequential versus random numbers is the
**random-account-number** configuration, not this screen. See
[Account number preferences](/help/system/account-number-preferences).

### External events

**System → External events** lists every outbound Fineract business-event
type and lets you enable or disable posting to Kafka or JMS. Membership
comes from `GET /externalevents/configuration`. This is not Hooks and not
the notifications bell. See [External events](/help/system/external-events).

### Entity-to-entity mapping

**System → Entity-to-entity mapping** lists Fineract office and role access
to loan products, savings products, and charges. Create, edit, or delete a
mapping; types come from `GET /entitytoentitymapping`. See
[Entity-to-entity mapping](/help/system/entity-mapping).

### System information

**System → System information** shows live build, server, and signed-in staff
facts: Bankayo version, Fineract actuator info, tenant header, server origin
and health, and office/roles from authentication. Fineract has no license API
— the screen documents that gap instead of inventing a license server. Any
signed-in staff member can open it. See
[System information](/help/system/information).

### Manage data tables

**System → Manage data tables** is the admin catalog for extra-data tables.
Create and edit use a three-step wizard (table, columns, review). Columns
are listed on the second step; add or edit a field in the docked side panel.
This is not the Extra data tab on a client, group, or center — that is where
staff fill values. See [Manage data tables](/help/system/datatables).

### External services

**System → External services** configures SMS, email (SMTP), Amazon S3, and
notification (GCM/FCM) gateways. Side tabs pick a gateway; the card shows
live fields. Leave secret fields blank to keep the stored value.
See [External services](/help/system/external-services).

### Hooks

**System → Hooks** lists Fineract webhooks. Create and edit use a four-step
wizard (details, config, events, review). Config fields and events come from
`GET /hooks/template` (Web, SMS Bridge, Elastic Search, Message Gateway).
Only Web can have more than one hook. See [Hooks](/help/system/hooks).

### Jobs: scheduler, workflow, and catch-up

**System → Jobs** can start or stop the global scheduler, run a job with
optional parameters, and show whether the batch queue is busy. A **Workflow**
tab orders close-of-business steps and can run loan COB for specific loan
IDs. Loan COB catch-up shows lag against the COB date and can be started
from this screen. See [Jobs](/help/system/jobs).

### Maker-checker flags

Which commands need a checker is now set under **System → Maker-checker**.
The flags are institution-wide (Fineract has no per-role maker-checker).
Role permission pages show a **Needs checker** badge on those commands.
See [Maker-checker](/help/system/maker-checker) and [Roles](/help/roles).

### Center meetings, collection sheet, accounts, notes, and extra data

A center workspace now has the same leftover sections as a group: a collection
meeting (inherited by groups in the center), a collection sheet for a meeting
date, a read-only accounts overview, staff notes, and extra data tables. Open
new loans or savings from a group, not from the center. See [Centers](/help/centers).

### Two-factor authentication

When the banking server requires two-factor authentication, sign-in asks for a one-time code after the password — sent by email or text, using the methods Fineract offers for that login. The token stays on the server (never in the browser) and is dropped when you sign out. See [Signing in](/help/signing-in).

### Clear the fund on a loan application

Fund on a loan application is optional. When the product has a fund, Bankayo
still copies it as the default, but officers can choose **No fund**. The same
clear option is on the loan product wizard. See
[Opening an account](/help/clients#opening-an-account) and
[Funds](/help/organization/funds).

### Fixed and recurring deposit charges, statements, and receipts

Fixed and recurring deposit workspaces now have **Charges** and **Statement**
sections, matching savings. Add, pay, waive, or stop fees on the account;
preview a date range and download a PDF; cash deposit and withdrawal rows (and
the sidebar after a successful cash posting) can download a receipt. Holds and
officer assignment are not on these accounts — Fineract has no commands for
them. See [Fixed deposit charges](/help/fixed/charges),
[Fixed deposit statement](/help/fixed/statement),
[Recurring deposit charges](/help/recurring/charges), and
[Recurring deposit statement](/help/recurring/statement).

### Open a fixed or recurring deposit

Opening a fixed or recurring deposit is a docked sidebar on the client's
list — the same pattern as savings, not a full-page wizard. Required is
the product, dates, amount, and term (plus collection frequency on a
recurring deposit). Optional starts from the product. See
[Opening an account](/help/clients#opening-an-account).

### Fixed and recurring deposit workspaces

A fixed or recurring deposit now opens in its own workspace — not under the
client header. Overview shows performance next to product and term facts;
Transactions lists postings. See [Fixed deposits](/help/fixed) and
[Recurring deposits](/help/recurring).

### Fixed and recurring deposit applications

Opening a fixed or recurring deposit now follows Fineract's account
create rules. A recurring deposit collects the recommended installment
and collection frequency (or inherits the group meeting calendar) —
not a single deposit amount. A fixed deposit still takes the deposit
amount and term, and can link savings for interest transfer and set
maturity instructions. Product min/max amount and term are enforced
before submit. See [Clients — Opening an account](/help/clients#opening-an-account).

### Fixed and recurring deposit product detail

Fixed and recurring deposit products open as a sectioned workspace, like
loan and savings products: general, terms, interest chart, and accounting
when the rule is not None. Minimum and maximum term show as a length and
unit together — for example **3 months**. See
[Fixed deposits](/help/products/fixed) and
[Recurring deposits](/help/products/recurring).

### Amount and unit on one field

Where a number only makes sense with its unit — minimum term, lock-in,
repay every, deposit period — Bankayo now shows them as one control, for
example **3 months**, instead of two separate boxes. Chart rate tiers
put the from-period and unit together the same way.

### Fixed and recurring deposit money movements

Recurring deposits use the [recurring deposit workspace](/help/recurring).
Fixed deposits use the [fixed deposit workspace](/help/fixed). On an active
account you can record a
deposit or withdrawal, calculate or post interest, and close early — with the
amount Fineract calculates on the chosen date, paid out in cash or transferred
to savings. Recurring deposits can change the recommended installment from a
date; withdrawals appear only when the product allows them. Matured accounts
close with the payout options Fineract returns (including reinvest when
offered). Cash deposit and withdrawal rows can be undone or modified. See
[Recurring deposits](/help/recurring) and
[Fixed deposits](/help/fixed).

### Templates

**Admin → Templates** is live. The list shows every user-generated document
and SMS template. Create and edit use a four-step wizard (details, mappers,
text, review). Entity (client or loan) and type (document or SMS) come from
the banking service. Click a Mustache parameter to insert it into the body.
See [Templates](/help/templates).

### Loan-loss provisioning documentation

Help now covers the full provisioning ecosystem:
[Loan-loss provisioning](/help/accounting/loan-loss-provisioning) (criteria,
entries, loans, GL accounts, and journal entries),
[Loan provisioning criteria](/help/organization/loan-provisioning-criteria),
and an expanded [Provisioning entries](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries)
page — each with a YouTube walkthrough on the help page.

### Manage reports

**System → Manage reports** is live. The list shows every report (core and
custom). Create, copy, and edit use a four-step wizard (details, parameters,
SQL, review). Core reports can only turn **In use** on or off.
**Copy** creates a custom report you can change SQL and parameters on.
New custom reports and **In use** control what appears in the
[Reports](/help/reports) catalog. See [Manage reports](/help/system/reports).

### Reports

**Reports** is live. The catalog lists published reports (search, type
filter, CSV/Excel). Open a **Table** or **SMS** report, fill its parameters,
and run it. Results page in a grid and export as CSV or Excel. Dates and
decimal amounts are formatted on screen (ledger figures with no currency
code; negatives in parentheses). Catalog and result **CSV** files are UTF-8
so Excel can open them; **Excel** downloads are real workbooks.
Table and SMS reports run from this screen. Chart, Pentaho, and BIRT
types may still appear when Fineract lists them; running those engines
is out of scope (Pentaho is a plugin; Chart and BIRT are not Bankayo run
targets). See [Reports](/help/reports).

### Faster entity search

**Ctrl+K** searches clients, loans, savings, shares, groups, centers, and client
identifiers in one go (name, account number, mobile, or external ID). Client
pickers in forms (for example guarantors and group members) also match account
number, mobile, external ID, and ID documents — not only display name. The
**Clients** list (and its CSV/Excel export) uses the same richer search when you
type in the filter box. See [Finding your way around](/help/navigation) and
[Clients](/help/clients).

### Client list shows mobile number

The client list (and CSV/Excel export) now includes each client's mobile number,
between name and external ID. See [Clients](/help/clients).

### Client wizard: default savings and the rest of create

**New client** can pick a **default savings product**. If you activate the
client now, Fineract opens an active savings account on that product as soon as
the client is created. If you leave them pending, the product is stored as
their default and the account opens when you activate them. The wizard also
collects the other create fields the API already accepted: gender and staff
flag for a person; constitution, business line, and incorporation for an
entity; optional account number and submitted date; client type and
classification; marital status and profession on family members. See
[Clients — Creating a client](/help/clients#creating-a-client).

### Share certificate

Active share accounts with approved shares can **Download certificate** from
the workspace header. The PDF names the member, account, product, approved
shares, and market value, with signature and stamp lines for the institution.
See [Shares](/help/shares).

### Savings statement and cash receipts

The savings workspace now has a **Statement** section: pick a date range, preview
opening and closing balances with credits and debits, and download a PDF. Cash
deposits and withdrawals (not reversed, not account transfers) can download a
receipt PDF from the transaction row, and from the sidebar after you record one.
See [Savings statement](/help/savings/statement).

### New client uses the product wizard layout

**New client** now uses the same full-page workspace as loan and savings
product wizards: close in the header, steps in a left rail on a large screen
(across the top on a tablet or phone), and **Previous** / **Next** (then
**Create client**) in the footer. The four steps are unchanged — basic
details, contact, optional family, then review. See
[Clients — Creating a client](/help/clients#creating-a-client).

### Fixed and recurring deposit product wizard

Creating or editing a fixed or recurring deposit product now matches how loan
and savings products work — each step validates before you move on, and a
final review summarizes everything before you save. The interest chart has
its own step, separate from the rest of the terms, and now supports multiple
rate tiers instead of a single flat rate — add a tier for each period range
that should earn a different rate (e.g. 4% for 3–6 months, 5% for 6–12
months), and tiers are checked for overlap as you go. Editing an existing
product with several tiers now correctly loads all of them, rather than only
the first. The terms step also gained a maximum term field alongside the
existing minimum. Recurring products put mandatory deposit and allow
withdrawal on terms (there is no separate settings step).

Accounting also has its own step now, with the same validation Fineract
itself applies: choosing cash or accrual periodic requires the matching GL
accounts (reference, control, transfers-suspense, interest, fee and penalty
income, plus fees/penalties-receivable and interest-payable for accrual) —
narrower than savings products since fixed and recurring deposits don't
support overdraft. Editing an existing product now correctly loads its
accounting setup too, which previously wasn't read back at all. See
[Fixed deposit products](/help/products/fixed) and
[Recurring deposit products](/help/products/recurring).

### Sign in to a server you manage

The sign-in screen can now connect to a Fineract server you specify, instead
of the one Bankayo ships with. Save a server as a named profile (just its
address and a tenant ID — leave off the path and Bankayo fills in the
standard one) from a panel on the sign-in screen, then pick it before
entering your username and password — your whole session runs against that
server. Only `https://` addresses with a valid, CA-issued certificate are
accepted; self-signed certificates are rejected. See
[Signing in](/help/signing-in).

### Share accounts

Share accounts open in their own `/shares/[id]` workspace (like savings and
loans), not under the client header. Overview shows holdings and market value;
**Transactions** lists purchases and redemptions, paged like savings and loans
(25 at a time; 10, 50, or 100 if you prefer). Reversed postings are hidden until
you turn on **Show reversed**. Approve or reject pending purchases from the row;
**Charges** lists fees. From **Actions** you can
approve, activate, undo approval, reject, close, apply for additional shares, or
redeem. Lifecycle commands use the Fineract date field names (`approvedDate`,
`activatedDate`, `closedDate`). See [Shares](/help/shares).

### Share products

Creating or editing a share product is an eight-step wizard (details,
currency, terms, settings, market price, charges, accounting, review). Terms
cover total shares, shares issued, unit price, and computed share capital.
Settings cover min / nominal / max shares, lock-in, a minimum active period
for dividends (days only), and dividends for inactive clients. Accounting is
None or Cash — share products have no accrual option. Cash needs share
reference, share suspense, share equity, and income from fees. Share
products cannot be deleted. There is no product start or close date. Open a
product for a sectioned workspace (general, terms, settings, plus market
price, fees, and accounting when present). A walkthrough creates **Normal
Share With Accounting** on Cash and edits it. See
[Share products](/help/products/shares),
[Create a share product](/help/products/shares/create), and
[Share product accounting](/help/products/shares/accounting).

### Find screens and sections

The sidebar searches every screen you can open — including nested Organization,
Products, and Accounting pages — without opening a section first. **Ctrl+K**
also lists the sections of the record you are on, and finds clients, loans,
groups, and centers. On a help page, the same shortcut (or the help sidebar
search) opens a topic list you can filter by Guides, Products, Accounting, or
Organization. See [Finding your way around](/help/navigation).

### Savings charges

The savings workspace has a **Charges** section. Add a fee or penalty from
savings charge templates, pay or waive an outstanding charge on an active
account, stop a recurring annual, monthly, or weekly fee, or remove a charge
from a pending application. Specified due-date charges need a due date; annual
and monthly fees need a due day. See [Savings charges](/help/savings/charges).

### Savings deposits and withdrawals

From a savings account you can record deposits and withdrawals, post or
calculate interest, hold and release amounts, block deposits or withdrawals,
and assign an officer. On a deposit or withdrawal you can undo it or change
the date, amount, payment type, payment details (account, cheque, routing,
receipt, bank), and external ID. A deposit or withdrawal that is an account
transfer is shown as an inward or outward transfer, with payment and transfer
details under the type; those rows cannot be undone or changed here. The
transaction list is paged (25 at a time; 10, 50, or 100 if you prefer). Reversed
postings are hidden until you turn on **Show reversed**. Cash
payments use institution payment types only — not system-generated ones. See
[Savings](/help/savings).

### Savings account workspace

A savings account has its own workspace — not the client header — with
performance (balance, available, deposits, inward and outward transfers when
they exist, withdrawals, posted interest, last activity), transactions, and
charges. Open it from a client or group. See [Savings](/help/savings).

### Open a savings account

Opening a savings account is a docked sidebar — product, submitted date, and
optional officer on Required, and product defaults you can override on Optional.
The same sidebar opens group savings. See
[Opening an account](/help/clients#opening-an-account) and
[Group savings](/help/groups#group-savings).

### Savings products

Creating or editing a savings product is an eight-step wizard (details,
currency, terms, settings, charges, accounting, mappings, review), the same
pattern as a loan product. Help now covers every field: compounding versus
posting (including daily posting and anniversary periods), daily versus
average balance, lock-in, overdraft and lien, withhold
tax, dormancy and escheat, attached charges, and cash or accrual GL accounts
with a glossary (savings control, savings reference, suspense, interest
payable, and the rest). The posting picker includes Daily and anniversary
periods, not only calendar monthly through annual. Open a product for a
sectioned workspace. Savings
accounting has no upfront accrual option and no product start or close date.
Two walkthroughs cover the wizard: create stops on **None** without saving;
[cash accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting) saves **Normal Savings
With Accounting**. See [Savings products](/help/products/savings),
[Savings product accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting), and
[Create a savings product](/help/products/savings/create).

### Centers

**Institution → Centers** lists village-style centers that hold groups.
Search by name, sort, and export. **New center** picks an office, optional
staff, and optional groups, and can activate immediately. Open a center for
overview and groups. **Actions** activate, close, or delete a pending center
with no groups. Groups must belong to the center's office and not already sit
in another center. See [Centers](/help/centers).

### Groups

**Institution → Groups** lists client groups. Search by name, filter by office,
staff, or external ID, sort, and export. **Import** downloads a template and
uploads a workbook. **New group** picks an office, optional staff, optional
center, and optional members, and can activate immediately. Open a group for
overview, members (roles and transfers), group and member loans and savings,
meetings, collection sheet, notes, documents, and extra data. **Open a group
loan** or **Open group savings** on an active group; **Open linked member
loans** or **Open linked member savings** when the group has members. Opening a
loan application shows the client's name or the group name in the
wizard header. Opening a savings account is a docked sidebar (required product and date,
optional product overrides), not a wizard. If the product sets a minimum or maximum principal or interest
rate, that range appears on Terms and you cannot continue until the amount is
inside it. Opening a group loan defaults the officer to that group's staff,
and can attach the collection meeting and link group savings.
**Actions** activate, close, assign or unassign staff, or delete a pending
group with no members. Members must belong to the group's office.
Joint-liability loans and savings still open from a member. See
[Groups](/help/groups).

### Provisioning entries

**Accounting → Provisioning entries** calculates loan-loss reserves from
your provisioning criteria. **New entry** picks a date on or before today,
and can post the journals immediately. Click a row to see the lines; create
journals or recreate the lines from the row menu while journals are not yet
posted. Posted journals appear under Journal entries. See
[Provisioning entries](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries).

### Accruals

**Accounting → Accruals** accrues loan income through a date before today.
**Run accruals** posts the journals; they appear under Journal entries. See
[Accruals](/help/accounting/accruals).

### Closures

**Accounting → Closures** locks an office so no journal can post or reverse on
or before that date. **New closure** picks an office and a date; comments are
optional. Click a row to change the comment (office and date stay as they
were). Delete only the latest closure for that office. See
[Closures](/help/accounting/closures).

### Accounting rules

**Accounting → Accounting rules** lists named debit and credit account pairs.
**New rule** picks an office and two detail GL accounts. Search, sort, and
export the catalog. Click a row to edit; delete from the row menu when the
rule is not in use. On **New journal**, pick a rule to fill the debit and
credit accounts, or choose the accounts yourself. See
[Accounting rules](/help/accounting/accounting-rules).

### Roles

**Admin → Roles** lists named permission sets. **New role** collects a name
and description, then opens that role so you can grant permissions. Click a
row to search and tick grants by group. Disable or delete is refused while
users still have the role. See
[Roles](/help/roles).

### Users

**Admin → Users** lists app logins (not Employees). Search, sort, and export
the catalog. **New user** opens a docked sidebar: username, name, email,
office, optional staff at that office, roles, and whether the password is
emailed or set here. Click a row to edit; leave password blank to keep it.
You cannot delete your own login or the system user. See
[Users](/help/users).

### Journal entries

**Accounting → Journal entries** lists debit and credit lines (last month
through today by default). Tabs switch all, manual, or system entries.
**Filters** sets dates, office, account, and transaction ID; **Clear**
restores the default month. **New journal** opens a wizard: details (optional accounting rule fills the
debit and credit accounts and shows them on that step), optional payment type
and cheque or receipt numbers, debit and credit lines, then review before you
post. Click a row to see the whole transaction and **Reverse** it when you have
permission. Amounts use the currency **code**. See
[Journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries).

### Teller cash allocate and settle

**Cash details** on a cashier is its own page. The header has a compact
currency picker (ISO codes such as `KES`, not a symbol) next to **Allocate
cash** and **Settle cash**. The page shows allocated, cash in, cash out,
settled, and net cash, plus a paginated transaction list. Allocate and
settle collect date, currency, amount, and a note. See
[Tellers](/help/organization/tellers).

### Organization offices, staff, calendar, and tellers

**Admin → Organization** now includes **Offices**, **Working days**,
**Employees**, **Holidays**, and **Tellers**. Offices are an expandable
branch tree (no delete), the same pattern as chart of accounts. Working
days is a weekday and repayment-reschedule form.
Employees are staff (no delete; deactivate is refused if they still have a
portfolio). Holidays start pending and need activate; delete is a soft
delete. Tellers are saved first; cashiers show on the list and open from
that column or the row menu. See [Tellers](/help/organization/tellers).

### Distinct navigation icons

Sidebar, Ctrl+K, and the Products / Organization / Accounting hubs now use a
different icon per screen: loan products are not the same box as charges, and
organization catalogs are not all a building. See
[Finding your way around](/help/navigation).

### Open a floating-rate loan

Officers can [open a loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account) on a product that
tracks a published floating rate. The wizard asks for a differential instead
of a nominal rate, and can keep the loan tracking later published periods when
the product allows it. The product’s fund is copied onto the application as
the default. See [Loan product terms](/help/products/loans/terms).

### Loan charges

[Charges](/help/products/charges) now documents every loan variation: five
collection times (disbursement, specified due date, installment, overdue,
tranche), five calculations including percent of disbursement on tranches
only, payment mode, penalty locks, min/max caps, tax, and how templates
attach to a product or to a loan. Income for loan charges is mapped on the
product mappings step, not on the charge.

### Loan product charges and mappings

The loan product wizard now has **eight** steps. Charges is a selectable table:
name and fee/penalty on the first column, with collection, calculation, and
payment mode as secondary text; amount (and caps) in the second; ledger
account and tax group in the third. Payment-channel, fee, penalty, and
reason-to-expense mappings moved out of Accounting onto their own
**Mappings** step. Channel mapping lists institution payment types only —
not system-generated types. See
[Create a loan product](/help/products/loans/create).

### Heritage theme

The account menu **Theme** list now includes **Heritage** (warm pearl and
heritage green, from the micropay UI). It is the default for new sessions.
Neutral, Royal Blue, Cerulean, Ruby, and Emerald remain. See
[Finding your way around](/help/navigation).

### Tax groups

**Admin → Organization → Tax groups** lists tax components and the groups a
charge can pick. Create a component first (name, percentage, optional start
date and credit account), then group components. Add or edit from a docked
sidebar. Fineract does not delete tax components or groups. Combined
percentages in a group cannot exceed 100%. See
[Organization — Tax groups](/help/organization#tax-groups).

### Write-off and charge-off reasons

**Admin → Organization → Loan reasons** lists the write-off and charge-off
reasons officers pick on a loan. Add or edit a reason (name, description,
position, active) from a docked sidebar. Delete asks for confirmation.
Fineract refuses a reason that is still in use. A loan product can map each
reason to an expense account when accounting is not None. See
[Organization — Loan reasons](/help/organization#loan-reasons).

### Chart of accounts

**Accounting → Chart of accounts** lists the ledger accounts a loan product
picks for cash or accrual accounting. Add or edit an account (name, GL code,
type, usage, optional parent header) from a docked sidebar. Detail accounts
show on product and charge pickers; headers group other accounts. The list now
supports expandable tree rows to show hierarchy inline. Delete
asks for confirmation. Fineract refuses an account that is still in use. See
[Accounting — Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).

### Organization delinquency buckets

**Admin → Organization → Delinquency buckets** lists day-past-due ranges and
the buckets a loan product can assign for installment-level delinquency.
Create ranges first, then group them into a bucket. Add or edit from a
docked sidebar; delete asks for confirmation. Fineract refuses a range or
bucket that is still in use. See
[Organization — Delinquency buckets](/help/organization#delinquency-buckets).

### Organization floating rates

**Admin → Organization → Floating rates** lists the published rates a floating
loan product can follow. Add or edit a rate (name, base lending rate, active,
and future periods) from a docked sidebar. Periods that have already started
cannot be changed. Officers can open a loan on a floating-rate product. See
[Organization — Floating rates](/help/organization#floating-rates).

### Organization payment types

**Admin → Organization → Payment types** lists the methods officers pick on
cash loan payments and that you map to fund-source accounts on a loan
product. Add or edit a type (name, description, cash flag, position) from a
docked sidebar. System-generated types cannot be deleted, and cash loan
payments never offer them. See
[Organization — Payment types](/help/organization#payment-types).

### Organization funds

**Admin → Organization → Funds** lists the donor, programme, or internal-pool
names you put on a loan product and when opening a loan. They tag whose
capital that product or loan uses; they do not hold a balance or post to the
ledger (that is the GL **fund source** account on accounting). Add or edit a
fund (name and optional external ID) from a docked sidebar. Fineract does
not delete funds. See [Organization — Funds](/help/organization/funds).

### Dedicated product help

Help for products is split by type. Loan products have a page per wizard
step (details through review). [Create a loan product](/help/products/loans/create)
embeds the public YouTube walkthrough. See
[Loan products](/help/products/loans).

### Command forms in a sidebar

Actions that collect details (a date, amount, reason, officer, and so on) now
open a docked sidebar over the page. Only yes/no confirms — delete, undo,
unassign — stay in a dialog. See [Clients](/help/clients#editing-and-status)
and [Loans](/help/loans#loan-details).

### Loan operations

The loan workspace **Actions** menu now covers the Fineract loan lifecycle:
approve and disburse, repayments and down payments, write-off, charge-off,
foreclosure, close, officer assignment, and the matching undo commands.
Disburse to a linked savings account is a choice on the disbursement form
when you have permission. Repayment can transfer from the customer’s
savings (shown first) or be recorded as cash; a transfer cannot exceed
available savings or the loan outstanding. Cash loan payments do not
offer system-generated payment types. What you see depends on the
loan’s status and your permissions. See [Loans](/help/loans#loan-details).

### Client loan list details

A client’s **Loans** section is a table: submitted date, then requested
amount. Outstanding balance and disbursement date are optional (outstanding
on by default; disbursement hidden). A loan in arrears keeps its status and
shows an **In arrears** badge. **Show closed** also lists a closed date.
Click a row to open that loan’s workspace. See
[Clients](/help/clients#client-details).

### Dedicated loan pages

Loans have their own list and workspace, like clients. Open **Loans** in the
sidebar, or click a loan on a client — the loan is no longer shown inside the
client header. Transaction lists are paged (25 at a time; 10, 50, or 100) and
hide reversed postings until you turn on **Show reversed**. A group loan names
the group and links to it. See [Loans](/help/loans).

### Open an account from a client

Active clients can open a loan, savings, fixed deposit, recurring deposit, or
share account from the matching section. Loans use a wizard with the product’s
defaults; savings, deposits, and shares are a docked sidebar. A loan
includes charges, the repayment schedule, and optional collateral and
guarantors before review. After submit you land on the new account. See
[Clients — Opening an account](/help/clients#opening-an-account).

### Account detail

Click a loan, savings, deposit, or share on a client to open it. Loans,
savings, shares, and deposits open in their own workspace. Approve, disburse
or activate, reject, withdraw, undo, or close from **Actions**. Loan repayments
and the rest of the loan lifecycle are on the loan workspace; savings deposits,
withdrawals, and related actions are on the [savings workspace](/help/savings);
share apply, redeem, and lifecycle actions are on the
[share workspace](/help/shares); fixed and recurring deposit money movements
are on the [fixed deposit workspace](/help/fixed) and
[recurring deposit workspace](/help/recurring). See
[Loans](/help/loans), [Savings](/help/savings), [Shares](/help/shares),
[Fixed deposits](/help/fixed), [Recurring deposits](/help/recurring), and
[Clients — Account detail](/help/clients#account-detail).

### Client associated records

Family, identities, documents, notes, and addresses on a client are no longer
read-only. Add, edit, or remove them from each section (addresses are
deactivated rather than deleted). Documents can be downloaded. A family
member with a date of birth also shows their age. Add and edit
open a docked sidebar. See
[Clients — Associated records](/help/clients#associated-records).

### Account menu

The top-right account control is an initials avatar. Open it for your name,
branch, roles, theme (Heritage, Neutral, Royal Blue, Cerulean, Ruby, or Emerald), appearance (light / dark /
system), and **Sign out**. Theme and appearance apply immediately. See
[Finding your way around](/help/navigation).

### Bankayo mark

Sign-in and the sidebar now use the Bankayo letter mark (the italic B / BANKAYO
boxes). The collapsed sidebar shows the B only. Sign-in uses a quiet animated
grid behind the mark on a large screen. See
[Signing in](/help/signing-in) and
[Finding your way around](/help/navigation).

### Loan product detail

A loan product now opens as a workspace with a section rail. Fees, penalties,
accounting entries, and payment/GL mappings appear as tables when the product
has them. Charge names link to the charge. See
[Loan products](/help/products/loans).

### Charge wizard

Creating a charge is now four steps (applies to, terms, amount & settings,
review). Options and required extras follow Fineract: charge time and
calculation depend on where the charge applies; loan and working-capital
charges need a payment mode; annual and monthly savings fees need a due day.
See [Charges](/help/products/charges).

### Loan product wizard

Creating a loan product is eight steps (details, currency, terms, settings,
charges, accounting, mappings, review). Charges is a selectable table of
amount, collection, calculation, payment mode, caps, tax, income account, and
related attributes. Mappings (payment channels, fees, penalties, write-off and
charge-off reasons) are their own step after accounting. The wizard covers the
combinations Fineract accepts: min/max principal and repayments, floating or
fixed interest, schedule type, grace, multi-disburse, guarantees, down payment,
variable installments, interest recalculation parameters, charges, and GL
accounts when accounting is not None. Progressive schedules send Fineract’s
default advanced payment allocation. Related fields share a row (min / default /
max, paired settings, GL accounts). See
[Create a loan product](/help/products/loans/create).

### Required fields

Forms mark required fields with an asterisk (*). A short note on each form
explains the mark, except sign-in, where username and password are enough.
See [Signing in](/help/signing-in),
[Clients](/help/clients), and [Products](/help/products).

### Products

**Admin → Products** now lists loan, savings, share, fixed deposit, and
recurring deposit products, plus charges. Create and edit use the same
full-page wizard. Only loan products have start and close dates. Creating or
editing a loan product on a large screen uses a two-column workspace (step
rail + form); on a tablet or phone the steps run across the top. Currency is a
searchable list (name and code) and can set decimal
places and multiples. Amounts such as principal show the currency **code**
in the field and stay disabled until a currency is chosen. See
[Products](/help/products).

### Product edit wizard

**Edit** on a loan, savings, share, charge, fixed deposit, or recurring
deposit product opens the same wizard used to create it. Close returns to the
product. Client edit stays a docked sidebar. See
[Products](/help/products).

### Edit client sidebar

**Edit** on a client opens a docked sidebar over the page you are on, instead
of sending you to a separate screen. Save and Cancel stay at the bottom of the
panel. Close it or press Cancel to stay where you were. See
[Clients — Editing and status](/help/clients#editing-and-status).

### Sidebar

The sidebar now leads with **Quick access** (Dashboard, Clients). Institution and

### Currency configuration

**Admin → Organization → Currencies** lists the currencies your institution
permits. Add from the full catalog or remove one that is not in use. Products

### Top bar

Breadcrumbs use the client's name instead of their ID, search finds clients as
well as screens (**Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**), and **?** opens help for the page you're

live in the account menu. The account control is your initials. See
[Finding your way around](/help/navigation).

### Client performance

Overview shows figures for the client: loan cycles, active loans, last loan
amount, total savings, shares, outstanding loan amount, and overdue loans.
Amounts use the currency code, not a symbol. Totals that have no single

### Client photo, signature, and closed accounts

A client's photo shows large in the header so staff can recognise them. Click it
for a closer look, or use the buttons under it to upload a file, capture from a

other product sections, **Show closed** includes closed accounts. See

### Client actions

### Client detail and associated lists

A client's page is now a tablet-friendly workspace: identity header, then

### Client registration wizard

Create clients directly from the client list: a four-step wizard (basic details,

[Clients — Creating a client](/help/clients#creating-a-client).

### Clients: list, search, and export

The first business module: browse your institution's clients under

bookmarkable — the URL always captures your current search and sort. See
[Clients](/help/clients).

The first release of Bankayo: sign in with your username and password, land on your
