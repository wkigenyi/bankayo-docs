Open **Institution → Clients** in the sidebar (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "clients").
You need client-viewing permission — if the menu item is missing, ask your administrator.

## The client list

Clients appear in a table showing account number, name, mobile number, external ID, status, and office.

- **Search** by name, account number, mobile, external ID, or ID document — results
  update as you type (same matching as client pickers elsewhere).
- **Sort** by clicking the Account No, Name, or External ID column headers.
- **Pages** — use the controls under the table; you can show 10, 25, 50, or 100 rows.
- **Open a client** by clicking anywhere on their row.
- The address bar always reflects your current view — **bookmark or share the URL** and
  it reopens exactly this filtered, sorted page.

## Exporting

The **Export** button downloads the *entire* filtered list (not just the visible page)
as a CSV or Excel file. Searching first narrows what's exported. Exports are recorded
for auditing.

## Creating a client

Press **New client** at the top of the client list (you need client-creation
permission to see it). That opens the same full-page layout as product wizards:
close in the header, steps in a left rail on a large screen (across the top on a
tablet or phone), and **Previous** / **Next** (then **Create client**) in the
footer.

Registration takes four steps:

1. **Basic details** — pick the office, choose whether the client is a **person**
   (first and last name, optional gender, and whether they are a staff member) or
   an **entity** (business name, optional constitution, business line, and
   incorporation details), and decide whether to activate immediately. Active
   clients can open accounts right away; you can also leave them pending and
   activate later. You can also choose a **default savings product**: if the
   client is activated now, Fineract opens an active savings account on that
   product immediately; if they stay pending, the product is stored as their
   default and the account opens when you activate them.
2. **Contact & identifiers** — all optional: an account number (leave blank for
   Fineract to assign one), submitted-on date, an external reference ID (e.g. a
   national ID or legacy system number), mobile number, email, date of birth
   (incorporation date for an entity), assigned staff, client type, and
   classification.
3. **Family** — optional: add spouse, children, or other family members (name,
   relationship, gender, marital status, profession, date of birth, dependent
   status). You can also skip this and add family later from the client's page.
4. **Review** — check everything, then **Create client**. You'll land on the new
   client's page.

Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). Each step checks your entries
before letting you continue, so mistakes surface immediately, not at the end.
You can jump back to an earlier step without losing what you typed.

## Client details

Open a client from the list. The page keeps their name, photo, account number,
status, office, external ID, mobile number, and email at the top, then splits
into sections so the associated lists stay usable on a laptop or tablet:

- **Overview** — performance (loan cycles, active loans, last loan amount, total
  savings, shares, outstanding loans, overdue loans), then identity (including
  name parts, gender, date of birth, and age when a date of birth is on file,
  and entity details), office, staff, classification, and important dates. Age
  also appears under the name in the client header. Amounts show the currency
  **code** (for example `KES 1,200.00`),
  not a symbol. When a total mixes accounts or has no currency of its own, the
  first currency enabled for the institution is used.
- **Loans**, **Savings**, **Fixed deposits**, **Recurring deposits**, **Shares** —
  one section per product. Loan rows show the account number, product, status,
  and submitted date, with requested amount at the end. Outstanding balance and
  disbursement date (actual when disbursed, otherwise expected) are available
  from a column header under **Columns** — outstanding is on by default,
  disbursement is hidden. A loan in arrears keeps its usual status and adds an
  **In arrears** badge; outstanding is also emphasized when that column is
  visible. Click a **loan** row to open that loan’s own workspace (not this
  client page). Click a **savings** row to open that savings account’s own
  workspace. Click a **share** row to open that share account’s own workspace.
  Deposits show the account number, product, status, and balance, and open on
  this client. Turn on **Show closed** to include closed
  accounts — the control then reads **Hide closed**, and loans also show a closed
  date. The address bar updates so you can bookmark that view.
- **Family** — household members recorded at registration or later. Add, edit,
  or delete members from this section. When a date of birth is on file, the
  list also shows their age.
- **Identities**, **Documents**, **Notes**, **Address** — records attached to
  the client. Add, edit, or remove them from each section (addresses are
  deactivated rather than deleted). Download a document from its row menu.
  The client's signature is managed under **Actions**, not here. Sections you
  don't have permission to see never appear in the menu.

On a wide screen the sections sit in a left-hand list. On a tablet they become a
top bar; if there isn't room, extra sections fold into a **+N** menu.

## Editing and status

From the client's header you can **Edit** identity and contact details. That
opens a docked sidebar over the client — Save and Cancel stay at the bottom,
and the rest of the page stays put. Required fields are marked with an
asterisk (*). Office changes use **Transfer**. Actions that need a date,
reason, office, or staff member also open a docked sidebar. Delete and
unassign staff ask you to confirm. Other actions depend on status and your
permissions:

- **Pending** — Activate, reject, withdraw, or delete.
- **Active** — Close (only if they have no open accounts) or transfer to another office.
- **Closed / rejected / withdrawn** — Reactivate or undo the previous decision.
- **Transfer in progress** — Accept, reject, or undo the transfer.

You can also assign or unassign staff. The client's photo sits in the header,
large enough to recognise them — click it for a closer look. Buttons under the
photo upload a file, capture from a camera, or delete. Signatures live under
**Actions**. Actions you don't have permission for never appear.

## Associated records

Family, identities, documents, notes, and addresses each have an **Add**
button when your role allows it. **Edit** and **Delete** (or **Deactivate**
for an address) sit in the row menu. Add and edit open a docked sidebar over
the list — Save and Cancel stay at the bottom. Required fields are marked
with an asterisk (*).

## Account detail

Click a **fixed deposit** row to open the [fixed deposit workspace](/help/fixed).
Click a **recurring deposit** row to open the
[recurring deposit workspace](/help/recurring). Click a **loan** row
to open the [loan workspace](/help/loans#loan-details). Click a **savings**
row to open the [savings workspace](/help/savings). Click a **share** row to
open the [share workspace](/help/shares).

Loan repayments and other loan operations are on the
[loan workspace](/help/loans#loan-details). Savings deposits, withdrawals,
[charges](/help/savings/charges), and other savings actions are on the
[savings workspace](/help/savings). Share apply, redeem, and lifecycle
actions are on the [share workspace](/help/shares). Fixed deposit deposits,
interest, and closure are on the [fixed deposit workspace](/help/fixed).
Recurring deposit installments, interest, and closure are on the
[recurring deposit workspace](/help/recurring). Deposit
[charges](/help/fixed/charges) and [statements](/help/fixed/statement) (including
cash receipts) are on those workspaces — the same pattern as savings. Fineract
does not expose hold or officer-assignment commands on fixed or recurring
deposits.

## Opening an account

On an **active** client, each account section has an **Open** button when your
role allows it. Loans open a full-page wizard (the header shows this
client's name). Savings, share, and deposit accounts open a docked sidebar
on their list.

- **Loan** — product (with optional external ID, account number, and note), dates
  (a standing instruction collects each installment from the linked
  savings when it falls due), then
  principal, interest, repayments, and product-gated extras (fixed EMI,
  equal amortization, monthly nth-day repayment, tranches, down payment
  percent and auto-repay, top-up, progressive-schedule fields when the
  product allows them), then
  charges, then the repayment schedule those terms produce, then optional
  collateral and guarantors, then review. Collateral rows pledge
  client-registered units of types from
  [Products → Collateral](/help/products/collaterals). A charge collected by account
  transfer needs a linked savings account. Group-only loans do not show
  linked savings. On the first step you can also assign an officer, a loan
  **purpose**, and a **fund** (the donor, programme, or internal pool that
  finances this loan). External ID is optional and sits beside the product, as a
  reference from another system. Purpose is always on this step and is optional — leave it
  blank if none apply, including when the list is empty. **Fund** is also always
  on this step and is optional — leave it blank if none apply, including when
  the list is empty. Funds are named under
  [Organization → Funds](/help/organization/funds). If the product has a fund,
  Bankayo copies it onto the application as the default; officers can still
  pick a different fund or **No fund**. A fund is not required. If the product sets a minimum or maximum **principal** or **interest
  rate**, that range appears under the field. You cannot continue until the
  amount is inside it. A floating-rate product asks for a **differential** instead of a
  nominal interest rate, and can track later published rate changes when the
  product allows it. The schedule step is a preview — recalculate after you change
  terms or charges if you want updated figures. It does not persist, and it
  does not send linked-account or standing-instruction fields. Fineract still
  calculates the repayment schedule when you submit.
  Collateral must already be registered on the client. Add a guarantor from
  the docked form: an existing client (optional savings hold), a staff
  member, or an external person (name required). Guarantors are saved after
  submit.
- **Savings** — a docked sidebar with two tabs. **Required** is the product,
  submitted date, and optional officer and external ID. **Optional** starts from
  the product (interest, balances, lock-in, overdraft, lien, tax, and charges)
  so you can override those for this account. Submit and Cancel sit at the
  bottom of the sidebar.
- **Fixed or recurring deposit** — a docked sidebar with two tabs, like
  savings. **Required** is the product, submitted date, optional officer and
  external ID, amount, and term. A **fixed deposit** needs the deposit
  amount. A **recurring deposit** needs the recommended installment and
  how often it collects (or inherit the group's meeting calendar). Amounts
  use the product's currency — there is no separate currency picker. The
  product's minimum and maximum amount and term appear under those fields.
  **Optional** starts from the product: interest compounding and posting,
  lock-in, withhold tax, and product charges. A fixed deposit can link
  savings so posted interest transfers there, and can set what happens
  at maturity (withdraw, transfer to savings, or reinvest when Fineract
  offers it). The product does not store a maturity instruction — leave
  **At maturity** as **Decide at close** to choose when the account is
  closed. Submit and Cancel sit at the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Share** — a docked sidebar: product, submitted and application dates,
  requested shares, and the savings account shares are paid from.

Close (X) on a wizard returns to the account list. **Back** and **Continue**
(or **Submit**) sit at the bottom of the page. A docked sidebar's Submit and
Cancel sit at the bottom of the sidebar instead. After submit you land on the
new account (a loan opens in its own workspace). The client must be active.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No clients yet" or "No matching clients" | Create a client, or clear the search box. |
| "Couldn't load clients" | Press Retry; if it persists, the core banking system may be unreachable — tell your administrator. |
| "Principal must be at least …" or "Interest rate must not exceed …" | Change the amount on Terms so it sits in the range shown under the field. |
| "Amount must be at least …" or "Term must be at least …" | Change the deposit amount or term so it sits in the range shown under the field. |
| "The loan was created, but one or more guarantors could not be saved" | The loan exists. The alert includes Fineract’s reason. Open the loan and add guarantors from there. |
| Clients menu missing | Your role lacks client-viewing permission. |
