title: Collection sheet
description: Find centers with a collection meeting and open the sheet to post repayments.
---

Open **Collection sheet** in Institution (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"collection sheet"). You need **READ_CENTER** to find meetings. Posting the
sheet uses **SAVECOLLECTIONSHEET_CENTER** on the center.

This is the institution-wide (productive) collection sheet. It is not the
collection sheet on a group or center workspace, and it is not the
[individual collection sheet](/help/individual-collection-sheet).

## What you can do

The meetings table is empty until you search. **Find meetings** opens a
sidebar: choose an **office**, **staff** member, and **meeting date**, then
submit. Fineract lists centers assigned to that officer that have a
collection meeting on that date, with due, overdue, and collected totals.
Open **Find meetings** again to change office, staff, or date.

Open a center name to go to that center's collection sheet, with the meeting
date filled in. Generate the sheet there, mark attendance, enter repayments
or disbursements, and save. See [Centers](/help/centers#collection-sheet).

## Empty results

Before you search, the table shows an empty state with **Find meetings**.
If no centers meet that day, check the date against the center's collection
meeting calendar, or open the center from **Centers** and use its collection
sheet directly.
