Once signed in, everything lives inside one workspace: a **sidebar** on the left for
navigation, a **top bar** with tools, and the page content in the middle. The
sidebar header is the Bankayo letter mark; collapsed, it shows the B only.

## Dashboard

Home is the **Dashboard**. Choose an **office** when you can see more than one;
the tiles follow that office. You get clients (all, active, inactive, new this
month), loan outstanding split into principal / interest / charges, overdue and
PAR 30, pending approval and disbursal, disbursed today and this month, expected
collections, savings balances, checker-inbox volume, offices, and staff. Each
tile opens the matching screen. **[Portfolio health](/help/portfolio-health)**
has PAR 60/90, product mix, and days-past-due bands. Tiles you are not allowed
to see stay hidden; if a figure cannot be loaded it shows as unavailable rather
than inventing a number.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is built for daily work first, then the rest of the institution:

- **Find a screen** — type in the box under the Bankayo mark. It searches every
  screen you can open, including nested ones under Institution, Accounting,
  Organization, Products, System, and Admin, so you do not have to open a
  group first. The box hides when the sidebar is collapsed; use **Ctrl+K** then.

- **Quick access** — Dashboard, Clients, and Loans, always one click away. **[Portfolio health](/help/portfolio-health)** sits on the root list when you can read loans.
- **[Income statement](/help/reports/income-statement)**, **[Balance sheet](/help/reports/balance-sheet)**, **[Reports](/help/reports)** (catalog and table runs are live), and **[Checker Inbox](/checker-inbox-and-tasks)** (pending maker-checker queue with approve, reject, and delete) sit on the root list.
- **Navigation groups** — Institution, Accounting, Organization, Products, System, and Admin. These are not pages; they open a list of screens (chevron on the right). Open a group to see its screens
  (Clients, Groups, Centers, Collection sheet, Individual collection sheet, Account transfers, Standing instructions, [Credit bureau](/help/credit-bureau), [Import savings](/help/savings/import), [Import shares](/help/shares/import); [chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts), [journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries), [loan-loss provisioning](/help/accounting/loan-loss-provisioning); [Users](/help/users), [Roles](/help/roles), [Templates](/help/templates); Offices, Working days,
  Employees, Holidays, [Tellers](/help/organization/tellers), Currencies,
  [Funds](/help/organization/funds), Payment types, Floating rates,
  Delinquency buckets, Loan reasons, Tax groups, [SMS campaigns](/help/organization/sms-campaigns), [bulk import](/help/organization/bulk-import) types (**Import offices**, **Import clients**, and the rest), [Bulk loan reassignment](/help/organization/bulk-loan-reassignment), [Password preferences](/help/organization/password-preferences), [Entity data-table checks](/help/organization/entity-data-table-checks), [Ad-hoc query](/help/organization/adhoc-query), [Standing instruction history](/help/organization/standing-instructions-history); [Loan products](/help/products/loans);
  [Manage reports](/help/system/reports), [Maker-checker](/help/system/maker-checker), …).
  The sidebar opens the group that matches the page you are on; **Back**
  returns to the root list.

Two things worth knowing:

- **You only see what you can use.** Menu items appear based on the permissions your
  administrator assigned to you — if something you expect is missing, ask them about
  your role.
- Screens that are not built yet show a **Soon** badge. You can still open them
  to see the placeholder.
- **Collapse it** with the toggle button at the top-left (or drag the sidebar edge).
  Collapsed, it shows icons only — hover to see names. Your choice is remembered.

## Help search

On any help page, the search box in the help sidebar (or **Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**)
opens a list of topics. Type to search the written pages — titles, headings, and
body — not the walkthrough videos. Tabs filter to **Guides**, **Products**,
**Accounting**, or **Organization**. **Esc** closes the list. From the rest of
the app, **?** opens help for the screen you are on; search help from there.

## Jump anywhere with the keyboard

Press **Ctrl+K** (or **⌘K** on a Mac, or click search in the top bar) to open the
quick-jump menu. Type a screen name to go there. If you are already on a client,
loan, savings account, share account, group, center, or product, the same menu
lists that record’s **sections** (overview, transactions, accounting, and so on).
Two or more characters also search clients (name, account number, mobile, or
external ID), loans, savings and share accounts, groups, centers, and client
identifiers — identifiers open the matching client.

## The top bar

- **Sidebar toggle** and **Home** sit in the left chrome.
- **Breadcrumbs** show where you are — including the client’s, loan’s, savings
  account’s, group’s, or center’s name —
  and let you step back up. On a narrow screen only the current page is shown.
- **Search** — the magnifying glass (or **Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**) opens the
  quick-jump menu.
- **Bell** — notifications, refreshed automatically about once a minute. Client
  notifications open that client's page; loan and savings notifications open
  that account.
- **?** — help for the screen you're on.
- **Account** — your initials. Open it for your name, branch, roles, theme
  (Heritage, Neutral, Royal Blue, Cerulean, Ruby, or Emerald), appearance (light / dark / system), and **Sign out**.
  Theme and appearance apply immediately and are remembered on this browser.
