Bankayo **customizes [Apache Fineract](https://fineract.apache.org/)** for your
institution: the operator workspace, the backend around the core, integrations,
and compliance — both your internal controls and the rules the bodies that
regulate you require — not only a new UI. This help center is **Fineract help**
for operators — it grows with every release, so if you can see a screen, there is
a page here that explains it.

Looking for **Fineract UI** or **Fineract customization** in one place? Start at
[Fineract help, UI, and customization](/help/fineract). Apache’s own platform docs
stay on the Apache site; this corpus is the operator path (permissions, templates,
and Bankayo screens).

## Getting around

- Use the sidebar to browse topics.
- Every screen in Bankayo has a **?** button in the header that brings you straight
  to the page describing it.

## Topics

- [Fineract help, UI, and customization](/help/fineract) — what this site is for,
  how Bankayo customizes Apache Fineract (UI, backend, integrations, and
  compliance with your internal controls and your regulators), and tenant-level
  configuration versus changing the Apache codebase.
- [Signing in](/help/signing-in) — add your Fineract server, sign in, two-factor
  authentication, session behavior, and troubleshooting.
- [Finding your way around](/help/navigation) — the sidebar, quick-jump search
  (Ctrl+K), notifications, and the top bar.
- [Dashboard](/help/dashboard) — office-scoped portfolio figures (clients,
  outstanding, overdue, PAR, operations). [Portfolio health](/help/portfolio-health)
  adds PAR 60/90 and days-past-due bands.
- [Users](/help/users) — app logins, offices, roles, and passwords.
- [Roles](/help/roles) — named permission sets assigned to users.
- [Templates](/help/templates) — Mustache documents and SMS for clients or loans.
- [Clients](/help/clients) — browse, search, sort, and export clients.
- [Groups](/help/groups) — browse, create, and manage client groups, including
  members, loans, savings, meetings, collection sheets, notes, documents, and
  extra data.
- [Centers](/help/centers) — browse, create, and manage centers that hold groups.
- [Collection sheet](/help/collection-sheet) — find centers with a meeting that
  day and open the sheet. [Individual collection sheet](/help/individual-collection-sheet)
  posts individual loan repayments and mandatory savings.
- [Account transfers](/help/account-transfers) — move money between savings
  accounts, or repay a loan from savings. [Standing instructions](/help/standing-instructions)
  schedule those transfers; [run history](/help/organization/standing-instructions-history)
  is under Organization.
- [Credit bureau](/help/credit-bureau) — map a seeded bureau to this
  institution, attach loan products, and store connection keys. Not a client
  credit profile.
- [Loans](/help/loans) — browse loans and open a loan workspace.
- [Savings](/help/savings) — open a savings account in its own workspace; record deposits, withdrawals, and related actions. [Savings charges](/help/savings/charges) covers fees and penalties on an account. [Import savings](/help/savings/import) bulk-posts deposits from a spreadsheet.
- [Shares](/help/shares) — open a share account in its own workspace. [Import shares](/help/shares/import) bulk-creates accounts or purchases additional shares from a spreadsheet.
- [Fixed deposits](/help/fixed) — open a fixed deposit in its own workspace; record deposits, interest, and closure. [Charges](/help/fixed/charges) and [statement](/help/fixed/statement) are on the account.
- [Recurring deposits](/help/recurring) — open a recurring deposit in its own workspace; record installments, interest, and closure. [Charges](/help/recurring/charges) and [statement](/help/recurring/statement) are on the account.
- [Organization](/help/organization) — institution settings, including [tellers](/help/organization/tellers), currencies, [funds](/help/organization/funds), payment types, floating rates, delinquency buckets, loan reasons, tax groups, [SMS campaigns](/help/organization/sms-campaigns), [bulk import](/help/organization/bulk-import) types, [bulk loan reassignment](/help/organization/bulk-loan-reassignment), [password preferences](/help/organization/password-preferences), [entity data-table checks](/help/organization/entity-data-table-checks), [ad-hoc query](/help/organization/adhoc-query), and [standing instruction history](/help/organization/standing-instructions-history).
- [Accounting](/help/accounting) — [chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts), [journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-entries), [import journal entries](/help/accounting/journal-import), [accounting rules](/help/accounting/accounting-rules), [closures](/help/accounting/closures), [accruals](/help/accounting/accruals), and [provisioning entries](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries).
- [Reports](/help/reports) — browse published reports and run table reports.
  [Income statement](/help/reports/income-statement) and
  [balance sheet](/help/reports/balance-sheet) are dedicated screens.
- [System](/help/system) — [manage reports](/help/system/reports),
  [configurations](/help/system/configurations), [codes](/help/system/codes),
  [maker-checker](/help/system/maker-checker), [jobs](/help/system/jobs),
  [audit trails](/help/system/audits), [hooks](/help/system/hooks),
  [external services](/help/system/external-services),
  [manage data tables](/help/system/datatables),
  [system information](/help/system/information),
  [entity-to-entity mapping](/help/system/entity-mapping),
  [external events](/help/system/external-events), and
  [account number preferences](/help/system/account-number-preferences).
- [Products](/help/products) — loan, savings, share, and deposit products, plus
  charges. [Loan products](/help/products/loans)
  and [savings products](/help/products/savings) have a page per wizard step,
  including [savings accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting) (cash,
  accrual, and every GL term). [Charges](/help/products/charges) covers loan
  fees and penalties; [savings charges](/help/savings/charges) covers the
  account.
- [What's new](/help/whats-new) — feature updates as they ship.
