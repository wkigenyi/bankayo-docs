The savings workspace has a **Charges** section. Open it from an account — there
is no institution-wide savings list. The **?** button on that section opens this
page.

A charge on an account is money: an amount due, paid, or waived. The template it
comes from lives under [Products → Charges](/help/products/charges). Collection
time and calculation are on that template, not here. A
[savings product](/help/products/savings/charges) can attach templates so they
start on every new account; you can still add more from this section.

The walkthrough on this page creates a **specified due-date** savings fee in the
catalog, adds it on an account, and pays it.

## The table

Each row is a charge on this account: name, fee or penalty, when it is
collected, due date, amount, paid, outstanding, and status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Outstanding** | An amount is still due. |
| **Overdue** | The due date has passed and an amount is still due. |
| **Paid** | Nothing is left to collect. |
| **Waived** | The outstanding amount was written off. |
| **Stopped** | A recurring annual, monthly, or weekly fee is no longer collected on this account. |

## Add a charge

**Add charge** opens a docked sidebar. You need permission to create a savings
account charge, and the account must not be closed or rejected.

Pick a savings charge in this account’s currency. Amount defaults from the
template and can be changed. Extra fields depend on collection time:

- **Specified due date** — a **due date**.
- **Annual fee** and **monthly fee** — a **due day** (day and month).
- **Monthly fee** and **weekly fee** — how often the fee repeats (months or
  weeks).

If the picker is empty, create an active savings charge first under
[Charges](/help/products/charges), in the same currency as this account.

Product-attached charges also appear when
[opening a savings account](/help/clients#opening-an-account) — already
selected; you can still add or remove charges after the account is open.

## Pay

On an **active** account, **Pay** collects the outstanding amount from this
savings account. The sidebar asks for a **payment date**, the **amount**, and
an optional **payment type** if your institution uses them. Cash types are
institution payment types only — not system-generated ones. Add those types
under [Organization → Payment types](/help/organization#payment-types).

You cannot pay a charge that is already paid, waived, or stopped.

## Waive

**Waive** writes off the outstanding amount so it is no longer due. The account
must be active and the charge still outstanding — the same rule as pay.

## Stop recurring

**Stop recurring** is only for an **annual**, **monthly**, or **weekly** fee
that has **no** amount still due. It stops collecting that fee on this account.
It does not appear on a specified due-date charge, or while an amount is still
outstanding — pay or waive first.

## Remove

**Remove** is only for a charge on a **pending** application that has not been
paid or waived. Confirm in the dialog. After the account is approved or
activated, you pay, waive, or stop instead of deleting.

## Savings collection times

These times are set on the [charge template](/help/products/charges), not on the
account:

- **Specified due date** — you pick the due date when you add it.
- **Savings activation** — when the account is activated.
- **Savings closure** — when the account is closed.
- **Withdrawal fee** — when the client withdraws. Pair with
  [Apply withdrawal fee to transfers](/help/products/savings/settings) if
  account-to-account transfers should pay it too.
- **Annual fee** / **Monthly fee** / **Weekly fee** — recurring.
- **Overdraft fee** — when the account is overdrawn.
- **No-activity fee** — when the account has no qualifying activity.

Calculation is **flat**, or **percent of amount** on withdrawal and no-activity
fees. Savings charges have **no** payment mode — that field is loan and
working-capital only.

A product-level annual fee on
[settings](/help/products/savings/settings) is separate from an annual-fee
**charge**. Use charges when you want the fee on this section. Do not set both
unless you intend two fees.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot add a charge | The account may be closed or rejected, or there are no savings charge templates in this currency. |
| There are no charges available to add | Create an active savings charge under [Charges](/help/products/charges) in this currency. |
| Cannot remove a charge | You can only remove a charge from a pending application that has not been paid or waived. |
| Cannot pay or waive a charge | The account must be active and the charge still outstanding. |
| Stop recurring is missing | That action is only for an annual, monthly, or weekly fee with nothing still due. |
