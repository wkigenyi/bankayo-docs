A savings account has its own workspace — not the client header or section list.
Open one from a [client’s Savings section](/help/clients#client-details) or from
[group savings](/help/groups#group-savings). The product that account was opened
on is defined under [Savings products](/help/products/savings); how it posts to
the books is explained under
[Savings product accounting](/help/products/savings/accounting).

The top of the page keeps the account number, status, account type (with an
icon), product, a link to the client or group this account belongs to, and the
balance, then splits into sections:

- **Overview** — performance (balance, available balance, deposits, withdrawals,
  posted interest, and last activity) next to product, interest rate, officer,
  and important dates. When they apply, you also see inward and outward
  transfers (account-to-account money, counted separately from cash deposits and
  withdrawals), interest not yet posted, overdraft used, tax withheld, fees, and
  dormancy. Amounts use the currency **code**. A group savings account also names
  the group and links to it.
- **Transactions** — postings on the account, 25 to a page (you can choose 10,
  50, or 100). Filter by transaction date and type; the address bar keeps those
  filters. Reversed postings are hidden until you turn on **Show reversed**.
  A deposit or withdrawal that came from
  an account transfer is labelled **Inward transfer** or **Outward transfer**;
  payment details and the transfer description appear under the type when
  Fineract sent them. You can undo or change a cash deposit or withdrawal (date,
  amount, payment type, payment details, and external ID). Account transfers
  cannot be undone or changed from this list — Fineract rejects that. On a hold
  you can release the amount. A cash deposit or withdrawal that is not reversed
  and not a transfer can **Download receipt** as a PDF.
- **Statement** — pick a from/to range (defaults to the last month through
  today) to preview opening and closing balances, credits, and debits, then
  **Download PDF**. See [Savings statement](/help/savings/statement).
- **Charges** — fees and penalties on this account. See
  [Savings charges](/help/savings/charges) for add, pay, waive, stop recurring,
  and remove.

**Actions** depend on status and your permissions. Commands that need a date,
amount, or other details open a docked sidebar over the account — the header
stays visible. Confirm-only commands (undo approval, calculate or post interest,
block, undo a transaction, release a hold) ask you to confirm. Cash deposits and
withdrawals list only institution payment types — not system-generated ones. Add
those types under
[Organization → Payment types](/help/organization#payment-types). After a cash
deposit or withdrawal, download a receipt from the sidebar when Fineract returns
the new transaction id.

Typical work from the savings header:

- **Pending** — approve, reject, withdraw, or delete the application; assign an
  officer.
- **Approved** — activate, or undo approval.
- **Active** — record a deposit or withdrawal, hold an amount, post or calculate
  interest, close the account, assign an officer, or block deposits, withdrawals,
  or both. A withdrawal or hold cannot exceed the available balance. If the
  account is already blocked, you see the matching unblock action instead.

If the account is inactive, dormant, blocked, or escheated, that state appears
next to the usual status.

From a client or group list, clicking a savings row opens this same workspace.

**Import savings** (Institution) bulk-posts deposits from a spreadsheet.
See [Import savings](/help/savings/import).

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Open a savings account from a client" | Click a savings account on a client or group. |
| Account missing or not loading | It may have been closed, or your role may not include savings — tell your administrator. |
