---
title: Configurations
description: Global enable/disable flags and institution-wide settings in Fineract.
---

Open **System → Configurations** (or **Ctrl+K** and type
"configurations"). You need **READ_CONFIGURATION** to view the list and
**UPDATE_CONFIGURATION** to change values.

## What you can do

- **Filter** by configuration name or enabled state.
- **Toggle** the switch on a row to enable or disable a flag immediately.
- **Edit** a row to change numeric, text, or date values in the sidebar.
- Switch to the **Business dates** tab to view or set the logical business date (when
  `enable-business-date` is on).
- **Ctrl+K** (or sidebar screen search): type **business date** to jump straight to
  that tab; on this page, **On this record** lists both tabs.
- **Export** the full list as CSV or Excel.

## Business dates

Use the **Business dates** tab on the same screen as global configurations. When
**enable-business-date** is off, the tab stays available with disabled controls and
a hint to enable the flag on the **Configurations** tab. When it is on, the tab
loads the current business and COB dates and **Set business date** opens the edit
sidebar.

## Maker-checker

When maker-checker is enabled on the tenant, configuration updates may queue
for approval in the [Checker Inbox](/checker-inbox-and-tasks) instead of
applying immediately.

## Locked settings

Some configurations show as locked after accounts exist on the tenant
(`trapDoor` in Fineract). Those cannot be changed from this screen.
