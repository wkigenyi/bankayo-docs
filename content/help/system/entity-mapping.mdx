---
title: Entity-to-entity mapping
description: Restrict which offices or roles can use which loan products, savings products, or charges.
---

Open **System → Entity-to-entity mapping** (or **Ctrl+K** and type "entity
mapping"). You need **READ_FINERACTENTITY** to view the list.
**CREATE_ENTITYMAPPING**, **UPDATE_ENTITYMAPPING**, and
**DELETE_ENTITYMAPPING** control the buttons. Superusers with
**ALL_FUNCTIONS** see everything.

These mappings limit which loan products, savings products, or charges an
office or role may use. Fineract applies them when
**office-specific-products-enabled** is on in
[Configurations](/help/system/configurations).

## What you can do

- **Browse** mappings by type (office or role to a product or charge), with
  optional from/to filters and name search.
- **Create** a mapping: pick the type Fineract offers, the from entity, the
  to entity, and optional start and end dates.
- **Edit** the from/to pair and dates. The mapping type cannot change after
  create.
- **Delete** a mapping from the row menu.
- **Export** the filtered list as CSV or Excel.

## Mapping types

Membership comes from `GET /entitytoentitymapping` — typically office access
to loan products, savings products, or charges, and role access to loan or
savings products. Bankayo labels those codes; it does not hide types Fineract
still returns.

From and to pickers load offices, roles, loan products, savings products, and
charges from their Fineract lists. Unknown types still appear in the table
using Fineract's code name.

A pair of from and to on the same type can exist only once. End date, when
set, cannot be before start date.

## Maker-checker

When maker-checker is enabled on the tenant, mapping changes may queue for
approval in the [Checker Inbox](/checker-inbox-and-tasks) instead of applying
immediately.
