---
title: Audit trails
description: Read-only history of commands that ran on the tenant — who made them, who checked them, and the payload.
---

Open **System → Audit trails** (or **Ctrl+K** and type "audit"). You need
**READ_AUDIT** to view the list and export.

This screen is **read-only**. It shows commands that have already been processed
(approved, rejected, or applied without maker-checker). Pending work still lives in
the [Checker Inbox](/checker-inbox-and-tasks).

## What you can do

- **Filter** by maker date range, maker, checker, action, entity, status, and
  resource, client, loan, or savings account IDs.
- **Open a row** to inspect the formatted command JSON in the detail sidebar.
- **Export** the filtered set as CSV or Excel.

## Distinction from Checker Inbox

| Screen | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Checker Inbox | Pending approvals — approve, reject, or delete |
| Audit trails | Historical log — browse and export only |
