---
title: Account number preferences
description: Choose how Fineract builds client, loan, savings, and other account numbers — prefix per entity.
---

Open **System → Account number preferences** (or **Ctrl+K** and type "account
number"). You need **READ_ACCOUNTNUMBERFORMAT** to view the list.
**CREATE_ACCOUNTNUMBERFORMAT**, **UPDATE_ACCOUNTNUMBERFORMAT**, and
**DELETE_ACCOUNTNUMBERFORMAT** control the buttons. Superusers with
**ALL_FUNCTIONS** see everything.

Each preference is one **account type** (client, loan, savings, center, group,
shares, or working capital loan — membership comes from
`GET /accountnumberformats/template`). Fineract allows only one preference
per type. New accounts of that type get a number built from the preference;
numbers already issued do not change.

## What you can do

- **Browse** preferences by account type, with optional search.
- **Create** a preference: pick an unused account type and an optional prefix.
  Prefix choices come from the template for that type (office name, product
  short name, client type, or a custom prefix). Shares have no prefix options.
- **Edit** the prefix. The account type cannot change after create. Fineract
  cannot clear a prefix back to none — delete and recreate if you need that.
- **Delete** a preference from the row menu. Existing account numbers stay as
  they are.
- **Export** the filtered list as CSV or Excel.

## Prefix and number body

The prefix is prepended to a numeric body. The body is either a zero-padded
generated id or a **random** number when **random-account-number** is on in
[Configurations](/help/system/configurations). Length follows
**custom-account-number-length** when that flag is enabled. This screen does
not toggle those flags.

A **custom prefix** (Fineract `PREFIX_SHORT_NAME`) also needs the prefix
characters themselves (up to 50). Fineract trims prefixes to 10 characters
when it builds the number.

## Maker-checker

When maker-checker is enabled on the tenant, preference changes may queue for
approval in the [Checker Inbox](/checker-inbox-and-tasks) instead of applying
immediately.
