Open **Organization** in the sidebar (a navigation group, not a page) or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "organization". You need permission for at least one
organization screen — if the group is missing, ask your administrator.

Organization lists institution settings you can use. Open a screen from the
group (or press **Ctrl+K** and type its name).

## Offices

Open **Offices** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"office"). You need office-viewing permission to see the list, plus create or
update permission for those actions. Fineract does not delete offices.

The list is an expandable **tree** under your hierarchy — same idea as
[Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts). Expand a parent to
see its branches. **Expand all** and **Collapse all** open or close the
whole tree. **Search** opens matching offices and their parents.
**Sort** and **export** (CSV or Excel) work like the other organization
catalogs.

- **New office** opens a docked sidebar. Name (up to 100 characters), parent,
  and opening date are required. External ID is optional.
- **Click a row** to edit. The **head office** cannot change parent.

The address bar is **Organization › Offices**.

## Working days

Open **Working days** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"working days"). You need working-days viewing permission to see the page, and
update permission to save.

This is a single settings form, not a list. Turn on the weekdays the
institution is open (at least one). **Repayment reschedule** is where a
repayment that falls on a closed day moves — same day, next working day, next
meeting day, or previous working day. Two optional toggles extend the loan
term when a daily repayment or a holiday repayment would otherwise shorten it.

The address bar is **Organization › Working days**.

## Employees

Open **Employees** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"staff" or "employee"). You need staff-viewing permission to see the list,
plus create or update permission for those actions. Fineract does not delete
staff — inactive members stay on the list.

- **Search** by name, office, mobile, or external ID.
- **New employee** opens a docked sidebar. First name, last name, and office
  are required. Loan officer, active, joining date, mobile, email, and
  external ID are optional. Mobile must be 7–15 digits, optionally with a
  leading +.
- **Click a row** to edit. Turning **Active** off is refused if that person
  still has a client or loan portfolio — the message from the banking service
  is shown; this screen does not force the change.

The address bar is **Organization › Employees**.

## Holidays

Open **Holidays** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"holiday"). You need holiday-viewing permission to see the list, plus create,
update, activate, or delete permission for those actions.

A holiday names a closed period for one or more offices and how loan
repayments that fall in it should move. New holidays are **pending** until
you **Activate** them from the row menu. Only **active** holidays reschedule
repayments.

- **Search** by name or office. Deleted holidays are hidden.
- **New holiday** opens a docked sidebar. Name, from/to dates, and at least
  one office are required. Reschedule is either the next repayment date or a
  specific date you pick.
- **Click a row** to edit. Pending holidays can change every field. Active
  holidays can change name and description only.
- **Delete** (row menu) asks for confirmation. This is a soft delete.

The address bar is **Organization › Holidays**.

## Tellers

[Tellers](/help/organization/tellers) are cash desks at an office. Save the
till first, then allocate **cashiers** (staff) from the list. **Cash details**
on a cashier is a dedicated page — allocate cash from the vault, settle it
back, and page through movements. Amounts use the currency **code**. Open
**Tellers** from this group (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "teller" or
"cashier").

## Currencies

Open **Currencies** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"currencies"). You need currency-viewing permission to see the list, and
currency-update permission to change it.

This list is the currencies your institution **permits**. Loan, savings, and
charge products can only be created in a currency that is enabled here. The
first enabled currency (when sorted by name) is the default when a screen
shows a total that has no currency of its own — for example mixed client
performance figures.

- **Search** by name or code.
- **Sort** by clicking Name, Code, or Decimal places.
- **Export** downloads the enabled list as CSV or Excel.
- **Add currency** opens a docked sidebar, searches the full catalog, and
  enables the one you pick.
- **Remove** (row menu) takes a currency out of use. You cannot remove the last
  remaining currency, and Fineract will refuse a currency that is still used by
  a product or charge.

The address bar is **Organization › Currencies**.

## Funds

[Funds](/help/organization/funds) are source-of-capital names — donor,
programme, or internal pool — that you tag on a [loan
product](/help/products/loans/details) and when [opening a
loan](/help/clients#opening-an-account). They are labels, not ledger
accounts. Open **Funds** from this group (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"funds"). The Fund field on those screens stays hidden until at least one
name exists.

## Payment types

Open **Payment types** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"payment types"). You need payment-type viewing permission to see the list,
plus create, update, or delete permission for those actions.

A payment type is a **method** — cash, mobile money, cheque — that officers
pick on cash loan payments, and that you can map to a fund-source GL account
on a [loan product](/help/products/loans/mappings). The payment-channel
mapping table and cash payment pickers stay empty until at least one
institution type exists here.

- **Search** by name or description.
- **Sort** by clicking Name, Cash, Position, or System.
- **Export** downloads the list as CSV or Excel.
- **New payment type** opens a docked sidebar. Name is required (up to 100
  characters). Description, cash flag, and position are optional.
- **Click a row** to edit that type.
- **Delete** (row menu) asks for confirmation. Fineract refuses a type that
  is still used on a payment or a product mapping.

**Cash payment** marks the type for reporting. Loan cash repayments list
every **non-system** type — cash or not. Fineract also creates
**system-generated** types (transfers, down payment, and similar). Those
appear with a System badge. You can rename them; you cannot delete them.
Cash loan payments and loan-product payment-channel mappings never offer
them.

The address bar is **Organization › Payment types**.

## Floating rates

Open **Floating rates** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "floating rates"). You need floating-rate viewing permission to see the
list, plus create or update permission for those actions.

A floating rate is a **published scheme** — a name, optional base-lending-rate
flag, and dated periods — that a [loan product](/help/products/loans/terms)
can track. The floating-rate picker on that step stays empty until at least
one **active** rate exists here.

- **Search** by name.
- **Export** downloads the list as CSV or Excel.
- **New floating rate** opens a docked sidebar. Name is required (up to 200
  characters). Add at least one period that starts **tomorrow or later**.
- **Click a row** to edit. Periods that have already started are read-only.
  Saving replaces only the **future** periods.
- Fineract does not delete floating rates.

**Base lending rate** may be on for only one scheme at a time. Other rates
can then mark a period as a **differential** against that base. Inactive
rates do not appear when you create a product.

Opening a loan on a floating-rate product uses that published rate plus the
product’s differential. Officers can keep the loan tracking later periods when
the product allows floating calculation.

The address bar is **Organization › Floating rates**.

## Delinquency buckets

Open **Delinquency buckets** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K**
and type "delinquency"). You need delinquency-bucket viewing permission to
see the list, plus create, update, or delete permission for those actions.

A **range** is a day-past-due band (classification, minimum days, optional
maximum). A **bucket** names a set of ranges. A
[loan product](/help/products/loans/settings) turns on installment-level
delinquency by picking a **bucket** — the field stays hidden until at least
one bucket exists here.

Start on **Ranges** if none exist yet:

- **Search** by classification (ranges) or name and range (buckets).
- **Export** downloads the current tab as CSV or Excel.
- **New range** opens a docked sidebar. Classification is required (up to
  100 characters). Minimum days must be at least 1. Leave maximum blank for
  an open-ended band (for example 181+).
- **New bucket** is available after at least one range exists. Name is
  required. Include at least one range. Fineract refuses overlapping ranges
  in the same bucket.
- **Click a row** to edit. **Delete** (row menu) asks for confirmation.
  Fineract refuses a range still used in a bucket, or a bucket still used
  on a product.

The address bar is **Organization › Delinquency buckets**.

## Loan reasons

Open **Loan reasons** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"write-off" or "charge-off"). You need code-value viewing permission to see
the list, plus create, update, or delete permission for those actions.

These are the write-off and charge-off reasons officers pick on a loan, and
that a [loan product](/help/products/loans/accounting) can map to an expense
account. The two lists are Fineract system codes (`WriteOffReasons` and
`ChargeOffReasons`); this page does not manage other codes.

- **Write-off** and **Charge-off** tabs. **Search** by name or description.
- **Export** downloads the current tab as CSV or Excel.
- **New** opens a docked sidebar. Name is required (up to 100 characters).
  Description, position, and active are optional. Inactive reasons do not
  appear when writing off or charging off a loan.
- **Click a row** to edit. **Delete** (row menu) asks for confirmation.
  Fineract refuses a reason that is still in use.

The address bar is **Organization › Loan reasons**.

## Loan provisioning criteria

Open **[Loan provisioning criteria](/help/organization/loan-provisioning-criteria)**
from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type "provisioning criteria").
You need provisioning-criteria viewing permission to see the list, plus create,
update, or delete permission for those actions.

Define overdue-age bands, reserve percentages, and liability and expense GL
accounts for each loan product.
[Provisioning entries](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries) need at least one
criteria before you can calculate reserves. See
[Loan-loss provisioning](/help/accounting/loan-loss-provisioning) for how
criteria, loans, GL accounts, and journal entries fit together.

The address bar is **Organization › Loan provisioning criteria**.

## Tax groups

Open **Tax groups** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"tax"). You need tax-group or tax-component viewing permission to see the
list, plus create or update permission for those actions. Fineract does not
delete tax components or groups.

A **component** is a named percentage (for example 16% VAT) with an optional
credit GL account. A **group** names a set of components. A
[charge](/help/products/charges) can pick a **group** — the field stays
hidden until at least one group exists here.

Start on **Components** if none exist yet:

- **Search** by name or account (components) or name and component (groups).
- **Export** downloads the current tab as CSV or Excel.
- **New component** opens a docked sidebar. Name and percentage are required
  (percentage greater than 0 and at most 100). Start date is optional.
  Credit account is optional and cannot change after create. Create those
  accounts under [Accounting → Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts).
- **New group** is available after at least one component exists. Name is
  required. Include at least one component. Combined percentages cannot
  exceed 100%.
- **Click a row** to edit. Changing a component percentage needs a start
  date tomorrow or later. You can add components to a group; Fineract does
  not remove a component once it is in the group.

The address bar is **Organization › Tax groups**.

## SMS campaigns

Open **SMS campaigns** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "sms campaign"). You need SMS-campaign viewing permission.

Compose SMS or in-app notifications against a Fineract business-rule
audience. Sending needs a configured SMS gateway (or the notification
toggle). See [SMS campaigns](/help/organization/sms-campaigns).

## Bulk import

Open an **Import …** type from Organization (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"import" plus the entity). You need the matching read permission for each
entity.

Download a Fineract Excel template, fill it, and upload it. This is the
organisation-wide importer — not the [journal preview importer](/help/accounting/journal-import),
the [savings deposit importer](/help/savings/import),
the [share purchase importer](/help/shares/import),
or the groups-list shortcut. See [Bulk import](/help/organization/bulk-import).

## Bulk loan reassignment

Open **Bulk loan reassignment** from the Organization hub (or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "bulk loan reassignment"). You need loan viewing
permission; submit needs bulk-reassign permission.

Move loans from one officer to another at the same office. See
[Bulk loan reassignment](/help/organization/bulk-loan-reassignment).

## Password preferences

Open **Password preferences** from the Organization hub (or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "password preferences"). You need password-preferences
viewing permission.

Choose which seeded Fineract policy new user passwords must meet. See
[Password preferences](/help/organization/password-preferences).

## Entity data-table checks

Open **Entity data-table checks** from the Organization hub (or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "entity data table checks"). You need entity-datatable-check
viewing permission.

Require a registered extra-data table when a client, group, loan, or savings
account reaches a status. This is not
[System → Manage data tables](/help/system/datatables). See
[Entity data-table checks](/help/organization/entity-data-table-checks).

## Ad-hoc query

Open **Ad-hoc query** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "ad-hoc query"). You need create, update, or delete permission —
Fineract has no read-only ad-hoc permission.

Saved SQL the schedule job inserts into a table. This is not a live
query console and not [Reports](/help/reports). See
[Ad-hoc query](/help/organization/adhoc-query).

## Fund mapping

Open **Fund mapping** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "fund mapping"). You need loan viewing permission.

Search outstanding loans grouped by office and product. This is not
[Funds](/help/organization/funds) and not a ledger mapping. See
[Fund mapping](/help/organization/fund-mapping).

## Investors

Open **Investors** from the Organization hub (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "investors"). You need loan viewing permission.

Search external asset-owner transfers. Cancel a pending or buyback
transfer from the row. Sale and buyback start on a loan. See
[Investors](/help/organization/investors).

## Loan originators

Open **Loan originators** from the Organization hub (or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "loan originators"). You need loan-originator
viewing permission.

Name parties that originate loans. Attach them on a pending loan, not
here. See [Loan originators](/help/organization/loan-originators).

## Standing instruction history

Open **Standing instruction history** from the Organization hub (or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "standing instruction history"). You need standing
instruction viewing permission.

This is every **run** of a standing instruction — successful transfers and
failures — not the instruction itself. Create and edit instructions under
[Institution → Standing instructions](/help/standing-instructions). See
[Standing instruction history](/help/organization/standing-instructions-history).
