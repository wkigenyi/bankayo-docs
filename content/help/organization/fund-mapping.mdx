---
title: Fund mapping
description: Search outstanding loans by office and product.
---

Open **Fund mapping** in Organization (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"fund mapping"). You need **READ_LOAN** (or **ALL_FUNCTIONS_READ**).

This is Fineract `GET /search/template` and `POST /search/advance`.
Mandatory body field is `entities`; Bankayo always sends `["loans"]`
because core Fineract only implements the loans query.

This is **not** [Funds](/help/organization/funds) (source-of-capital
names) and **not** [Financial activity mappings](/help/accounting/financial-activities)
(fund-source GL accounts). The Mifos name is leftover from an
outstanding-by-office-and-product search.

## What you can do

- Choose a **date type** and range. Date types are Fineract
  `SearchLoanDate` (`approvalDate`, `createdDate`, `disbursalDate`). The
  template does not return them.
- Optionally restrict **status**, **loan products**, and **offices**.
  Products and offices come from `GET /search/template` (R8). Status
  codes are the strings Fineract maps (`all`, `active`, `overpaid`,
  `closed` = obligations met, `writeoff` = written-off). `arrears` is
  omitted — Fineract’s branch does not add a loan status.
- Optionally filter the grouped result by **share of outstanding**
  (percent) or **outstanding amount**, using Fineract’s comparison
  operators (`between`, `<=`, `>=`, `<`, `>`, `=`).
- **Search** lists one row per office and loan product: loan count,
  outstanding, and that group’s share of expected repayment. Export the
  last search as CSV or Excel.

Amounts have no currency code. Fineract sums `total_outstanding_derived`
without one — same idea as ledger amounts on table reports.
