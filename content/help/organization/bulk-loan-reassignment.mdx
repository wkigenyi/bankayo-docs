---
title: Bulk loan reassignment
description: Move loans from one loan officer to another at the same office.
---

Open **Bulk loan reassignment** in Organization (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "bulk loan reassignment"). You need **READ_LOAN** to open the screen
and **BULKREASSIGN_LOAN** to submit.

This is Fineract `GET/POST /loans/loanreassignment`. It is not assigning
an officer on a single loan workspace, and not [bulk import](/help/organization/bulk-import)
of loan accounts.

## What you can do

Pick an **office**. Fineract then lists loan officers at that office.
Pick the **from** officer to load their client and group loans, then the
**to** officer (everyone except the from officer) and an **assignment
date**.

Select one or more loans and **Reassign**. Fineract moves each selected
loan if the from officer still holds it and the client or group is
active. Hard-locked loans are refused by Fineract — Bankayo shows that
reason.

Offices and officers come from the reassignment template, not a frozen
staff list.
