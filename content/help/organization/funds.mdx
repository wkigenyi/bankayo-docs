Open **Funds** from **Organization** (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"funds"). You need fund-viewing permission to see the list, create permission
to add a fund, and update permission to edit one.

A fund is a **source-of-funds name**: the donor, government programme, or
internal pool that put up the money. It is a tag, not an account. It does
not hold a balance and it does not post to the general ledger. You put it
on a [loan product](/help/products/loans/details) and, separately, on a loan
when [opening](/help/clients#opening-an-account). Fineract then stores whose
capital that product or loan uses.

The Fund field on those screens appears only after at least one fund exists
here. If you never split the book by who financed it, you can leave this
list empty.

## Why they help

Without funds, products and loans do not record who supplied the capital.
With a short catalog you can:

- Separate **own capital** from **donor** or **government** money — for
  example Internal, World Bank SME 2026, or a youth programme.
- Give a product a programme identity. After save, that name shows under
  **General** on the [product](/help/products/loans).
- Tag an individual loan when an officer opens it, even if several funds
  share the same product terms.
- Store an **external ID** that matches a donor project code or another
  system, so the name here lines up with theirs.
- **Export** the catalog for audit.

Bankayo copies the product’s fund onto a new loan as the default.
Officers can still pick a different fund, or **No fund** — a fund is not
required on the application.

## Not the same as a fund-source account

**Fund source** on [loan product accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting)
is a **GL account** — the cash or asset account disbursements and repayments
post through. That is bookkeeping. An Organization fund is only the
programme name. You can map many loans to one GL fund-source account and
still tag them with different Organization funds.

**Hold guarantee funds** on [loan product settings](/help/products/loans/settings)
is a different feature (money held against a loan). It does not use this
list.

## The list and creating a fund

- **Search** by name or external ID.
- **Sort** by clicking a column heading.
- **Export** downloads the list as CSV or Excel.
- **New fund** opens a docked sidebar. Name is required (up to 100
  characters). External ID is optional, for a reference from another system.
- **Click a row** to edit that fund. The name updates everywhere the fund is
  used.

Fineract does not delete funds. If a name is no longer used, rename it or
leave it unused.

The address bar is **Organization › Funds**.
