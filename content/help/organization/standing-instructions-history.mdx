---
title: Standing instruction history
description: Runs of standing instructions — successful transfers and failures.
---

Open **Standing instruction history** in Organization (or press **Ctrl+K**
and type "standing instruction history"). You need
**READ_STANDINGINSTRUCTION**. Create and edit instructions under
[Institution → Standing instructions](/help/standing-instructions).

This list is every **run** of a standing instruction in the date range you
choose — not the instruction itself. A failed run shows the error Fineract
returned.

## What you can do

**Filters** narrow by client name, transfer type (from the standing
instruction template), and executed date range. **Export** downloads the
filtered runs as CSV or Excel. Open a row to see the instruction and its
associated transfers.

This is not [Account transfers](/help/account-transfers) (one-off postings)
and not the associated-transfers grid on an instruction.
