title: SMS campaigns
description: Compose and send SMS or in-app notifications from a Fineract business-rule audience.
---

Open **SMS campaigns** in Organization (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"sms campaign"). You need **READ_SMSCAMPAIGN**. Create, edit, activate,
close, reactivate, and delete need the matching `*_SMSCAMPAIGN`
permissions. The message log on a campaign needs **READ_SMS**.

This is the core Fineract campaign API (`/smscampaigns`). Messages leave only
when an [SMS gateway](/help/system/external-services) is configured (or you
send an in-app notification). If the gateway is missing or not running,
Bankayo still lists and opens campaigns and shows a banner instead of failing
the page. Create, preview, and send wait until the gateway is up.

This is not [Templates](/help/templates) (Mustache document/SMS text) and
not a one-off SMS from a client workspace.

## What you can do

**New campaign** is a four-step wizard: details, audience, message, review.

- **Trigger** membership comes from the campaign template (Direct, Schedule,
  or Triggered). Triggered rules are the SMS reports whose subtype is
  Triggered; the others are Direct and Schedule.
- **Business rule** is a SMS report. Audience parameters are that report's
  stretchy parameters. Triggered campaigns do not require them.
- **Schedule** campaigns collect frequency, interval, optional weekday, and
  first-run date. Frequency and weekdays come from the template (R8).
- **Message** is at most 480 characters. **Preview recipients** calls
  `POST /smscampaigns/preview` when the trigger is not Triggered.

A new campaign is **pending**. **Activate** (with a date) sends a Direct
campaign immediately or starts a Schedule. **Close** stops it.
**Reactivate** is only for a closed campaign. **Edit** and **Delete** are
only while closed — Fineract refuses otherwise.

**Export** downloads the campaign list as CSV or Excel.
