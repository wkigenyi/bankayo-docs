Open **Loan provisioning criteria** from **Organization** (or press
**Ctrl+K** and type "provisioning criteria"). You need
`READ_PROVISIONINGCRITERIA` to see the list, plus create, update, or delete
permission for those actions.

Criteria define **how much to reserve** and **which GL accounts** to use for
each overdue-day band. [Provisioning entries](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries)
need at least one criteria before Fineract will calculate reserves. For the
full picture — loans, chart of accounts, entries, and journals — see
[Loan-loss provisioning](/help/accounting/loan-loss-provisioning).

## Prerequisites

- **[Loan products](/help/products/loans)** must exist. Each criteria binds
  one or more products; only loans on those products are included in
  provisioning runs.
- **[Chart of accounts](/help/accounting/chart-of-accounts)** needs **detail**
  GL accounts:
  - **Liability** accounts (type Liability) for the balance-sheet provision.
  - **Expense** accounts (type Expense) for the reserve charge.
  These are **not** the fund source, loan portfolio, or income accounts on
  [loan product accounting](/help/products/loans/accounting).

Fineract supplies **provisioning categories** (for example STANDARD,
SUB-STANDARD, DOUBTFUL, LOSS) from
`GET /provisioningcriteria/template`. You cannot add or rename categories in
Bankayo — you configure each one inside the criteria.

## The list

- **Search** by criteria name or who created it.
- **New criteria** opens the create wizard.
- **Click a row** to edit (single-page form, not the wizard).
- **Delete** (row menu) asks for confirmation. Fineract refuses criteria still
  referenced by a [provisioning entry](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries).

The address bar is **Organization › Loan provisioning criteria**.

## Creating criteria (wizard)

**New criteria** at `/organization/loan-provisioning-criteria/new` walks
through:

### Setup

- **Name** — up to 100 characters.
- **Loan products** — pick every product this criteria should cover (at least
  one).

### One step per category

Fineract categories appear in template order. On each step:

- **Min age** — overdue days at the start of the band.
  - The **first** category (usually STANDARD): you enter min age; it typically
    starts at **0**.
  - **Later categories**: min age is **automatic** — one day after the previous
    category’s max age. Bands must be **contiguous** (no gaps, no overlaps).
- **Max age** — last overdue day in this band (required).
- **Reserve %** — provisioning percentage from 0 to 100.
- **Liability GL** — liability detail account from the chart.
- **Expense GL** — expense detail account from the chart.

The stepper marks steps with validation issues after you try to reach **Review**
or **Create**, so you can jump back without visiting every step.

### Review

Confirm name, products, and every category band before **Create criteria**.

## Editing criteria

**Click a row** to open `/organization/loan-provisioning-criteria/[id]/edit`.

The edit form shows all categories in one **table** with the same fields. Min
age on the first row is editable; later rows show the derived min age. Changing
a **max age** updates the next row’s min age automatically.

Save when you adjust bands, percentages, products, or GL accounts. If criteria
already fed an unposted provisioning entry, use **Recreate lines** on that
entry after you save.

## Age bands (contiguous)

Provisioning categories are **ordered**. If STANDARD ends at day 30,
SUB-STANDARD starts at day **31**, not 32 or 30.

| Category | Min age | Max age | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STANDARD | 0 | 30 | 0–30 days past due |
| SUB-STANDARD | 31 | 60 | 31–60 days past due |
| DOUBTFUL | 61 | 90 | 61–90 days past due |
| LOSS | 91 | 360 | 91–360 days past due |

Reserve percentages often increase as delinquency worsens (for example 2% →
20% → 100%). Fineract validates overlap at the engine level; Bankayo also
enforces contiguity in the form.

## GL account rules

- **Liability GL** pickers list **liability** detail accounts only.
- **Expense GL** pickers list **expense** detail accounts only.
- Options come from the provisioning template first; if empty, Bankayo loads
  eligible accounts from the chart (manual entries allowed, not disabled,
  detail usage).

Each category can use different accounts. Many institutions reuse the same
pair across bands for simplicity.

## What happens after you save

Fineract stores the criteria. On the next [provisioning
entry](/help/accounting/provisioning-entries) for a date, the engine:

1. Considers loans on the bound products.
2. Matches each loan’s overdue days to a category band.
3. Applies the reserve percentage and GL mapping from that band.

You do **not** pick a criteria on the entry screen — Fineract uses all
configured criteria.
