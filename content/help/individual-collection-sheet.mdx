---
title: Individual collection sheet
description: Bulk repayments and mandatory savings for individual loans in an office.
---

Open **Individual collection sheet** in Institution (or press **Ctrl+K** and
type "individual collection sheet"). You need **READ_COLLECTIONSHEET** to
generate the sheet and **SAVE_COLLECTIONSHEET** to post it.

This is for individual (non-JLG) loans and mandatory savings. Center and
group meetings use [Collection sheet](/help/collection-sheet) or the sheet
on a [center](/help/centers#collection-sheet) or
[group](/help/groups#collection-sheet).

## What you can do

The loans and savings tables are empty until you generate a sheet.
**Generate sheet** opens a sidebar: choose an **office** and **transaction
date**. **Staff** is optional and narrows the sheet to that officer. Submit
to load loans due for repayment or disbursement and mandatory savings due
that day. Open **Generate sheet** again to change office, staff, or date.

Enter amounts on the loan and savings tables. Leave a disbursement blank
when the loan is not ready to disburse. **Save collection sheet** (on the
results toolbar) posts repayments, disbursements, and savings deposits.
Secret or payment-detail fields are not required; Fineract accepts the
amounts on their own.

## Empty results

Before you generate, the table shows an empty state with **Generate sheet**.
If nothing is due, there are no individual loans or mandatory savings for
that office (and staff, if chosen) on that date.
