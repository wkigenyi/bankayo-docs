---
title: Account transfers
description: Move money between savings accounts, or repay a loan from savings.
---

Open **Account transfers** in Institution (or press **Ctrl+K** and type
"account transfer"). You need **READ_ACCOUNTTRANSFER** to see the list and
**CREATE_ACCOUNTTRANSFER** to post a transfer. Reverse a posted transfer with
**UNDO_ACCOUNTTRANSFER**.

This is the institution-wide transfer catalog. It is not the
**Repay from savings** action on a [loan](/help/loans) workspace, and not the
inward or outward transfer rows on a [savings](/help/savings) account.

## What you can do

The list shows posted transfers. **Export** downloads the page as CSV or
Excel. **New transfer** opens a four-step wizard: from account, to account,
details, review.

Pick the **from** client first. Fineract fills the office from that client
(the transfer template cannot start from office alone). Then pick account
type (savings or loan) and the account. The **to** side works the same way.
Amount must be greater than zero. Description is required and at most 200
characters. Dates on screen are ISO `yyyy-MM-dd`; the banking service
receives them in locale `en`.

Open a row to see both sides. **Undo transfer** reverses the posting on both
accounts when you have undo permission.
